Chapter summary

Vision and Hearing

Formulae and terms for a final review.

AKey relationships

  • Lens power: \(P=1/f\), with \(f\) in metres.
  • Thin lenses in contact: \(P_{total}=P_1+P_2\).
  • Lens formula: \(P=1/u+1/v\).
  • Eye resolution: \(\theta_{min}\approx1.22\lambda/D\).
  • Small angle: \(\ell\approx r\theta\).
  • Sound level: \(L=10\log_{10}(I/I_0)\).
  • Sound intensity: \(I=P/A\); point-source intensity varies as \(1/r^2\).
  • Middle-ear pressure gain: approximately \(1.3\times17\approx22\).

BDefinitions

  • Accommodation: changing lens shape to focus objects at different distances.
  • Near point: closest point that can be focused clearly.
  • Far point: farthest point that can be focused clearly.
  • Dioptre: unit of lens power, equal to m⁻¹.
  • Resolving power: ability to distinguish close details.
  • Basilar membrane: cochlear membrane whose position of peak vibration encodes frequency.
  • Phon: loudness level equal to the dB level of an equally loud 1 kHz tone.
