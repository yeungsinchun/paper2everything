AImaging waves

Imaging methods turn a wave signal into a picture of internal structure or function. Ultrasound and endoscopy use non-ionizing waves. Radiography and CT use X-rays; radionuclide imaging detects gamma rays from a tracer.

MethodSignalWhat is measured
Ultrasoundsound echoesboundaries between tissues
Endoscopyvisible lightreflected light from a surface

Radionuclide imagingemitted gamma raystracer distribution and function
Definition

Non-invasive means no instrument or tracer enters the body. It does not mean risk-free: X-rays are non-invasive but ionizing.

Quick check

Which statement about a non-invasive scan is correct?

Non-invasive describes access to the body. An X-ray radiograph is non-invasive but uses ionizing radiation.

BResolution and trade-offs

Shorter wavelengths generally diffract less, but image quality also depends on detector spacing and how the signal is processed. The best method depends on the target, risk, cost and time.

Quick check

What wave effect fundamentally limits resolution?

Diffraction spreads waves around an aperture, limiting the separation at which two details remain distinct.
