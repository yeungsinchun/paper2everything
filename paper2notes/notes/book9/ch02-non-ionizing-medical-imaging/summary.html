Non-ionizing Medical Imaging

Formulae and terms for a final review.

AKey relationships

  • Z = ρc
  • α = ((Z₂ − Z₁)/(Z₂ + Z₁))²
  • s = ct/2
  • sin c = n₂/n₁
  • sin θmax = ncore cos c

BDefinitions

  • Acoustic impedance: density × sound speed.
  • Reflection coefficient: incident intensity fraction reflected.
  • Pulse-echo: depth from half the out-and-back distance.
  • Critical angle: incidence angle whose refracted ray grazes the boundary.
  • Coherent bundle: fibres preserve their relative order.
