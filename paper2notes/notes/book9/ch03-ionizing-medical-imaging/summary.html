Formulae and terms for a final review.

AKey relationships

  • I = I₀e^(−μx)

  • I/I₀ = (1/2)ⁿ

  • E = D wR wT

BDefinitions

  • Attenuation: reduction of beam intensity in matter.
  • Half-value thickness: thickness that halves intensity.
  • Tracer: radionuclide attached to a carrier for target tissue.
  • Effective half-life: combined radioactive decay and biological removal.
  • Effective dose: absorbed dose weighted by radiation and tissue.
