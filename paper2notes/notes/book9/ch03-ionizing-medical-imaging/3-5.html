AChoose an imaging method

NeedMethod often suitedWhy
Foetus or moving soft tissueUltrasoundreal-time; non-ionizing
Broken boneRadiographfast projection with strong bone contrast
Cross-sectional anatomyCTreconstructs a slice from many angles
Organ functionRadionuclide imagingtracer distribution shows activity
Internal surfaceEndoscopyvisible-light view through a scope

Method selection depends on the clinical question, depth and access, required detail, risks, availability and time. A method suited to one target may be unsuitable for another.

Quick check

Which method best shows the distribution of a tracer used to assess organ function?

The emitted photons reveal where the tracer accumulates and how its distribution changes.

Quick check

Which statement is the soundest general rule?

The clinical question, method limits and patient risk all matter; one modality is not best for every case.
