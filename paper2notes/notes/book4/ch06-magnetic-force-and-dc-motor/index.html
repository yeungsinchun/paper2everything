Magnetic Force and the DC Motor

Force on a wire, force on a charge and the motor. Prior knowledge: only force and motion. Every field and circuit idea is taught from zero with a visual and a check.

Learning objectives

Students should be able to:

  • examine the force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field and determine relative directions of \(F\), \(B\) and \(I\)

  • determine factors affecting the force on a straight wire and represent it by \(F = B I l \sin\theta\)

  • represent the force on a moving charge by \(F = B Q v \sin\theta\) and solve problems

  • solve problems involving current-carrying conductors in a magnetic field

  • determine the turning effect on a current-carrying coil in a magnetic field

  • describe the structure of a simple d.c. motor and how it works
