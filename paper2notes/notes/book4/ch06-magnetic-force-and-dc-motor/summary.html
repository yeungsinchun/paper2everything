Which statement best fits this chapter's theme?

B. Definitions (R=V/I, E=F/q, Φ=BAcosθ) hold generally; laws like Ohm, uniform E=V/d, ideal transformer are conditional. A confuses uniform vs non-uniform; C ignores R load; D swaps Lenz (direction) with Faraday (magnitude).
