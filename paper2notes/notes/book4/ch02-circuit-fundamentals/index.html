Book 4 · Ch.2

Circuit Fundamentals

Current, potential difference, resistance and resistivity. Prior knowledge: only force and motion. Every field and circuit idea is taught from zero with a visual and a check.

Learning objectives

Students should be able to:

  • define electric current as the rate of flow of charge \(I = \dfrac{\Delta Q}{\Delta t}\) and state the convention for its direction

  • describe energy transformations in electric circuits

  • define potential difference between two points as the energy converted per unit charge outside the source

  • define e.m.f. as the energy imparted by the source per unit charge passing through it

  • define resistance \(R = \dfrac{V}{I}\)

  • describe variation of current with p.d. for metal wires, electrolytes, filament lamps and diodes

  • realise Ohm's law as a special case

  • determine factors affecting resistance of a wire and define resistivity \(\rho = \dfrac{R A}{l}\)

  • describe the effect of temperature on resistance of metals and semiconductors
