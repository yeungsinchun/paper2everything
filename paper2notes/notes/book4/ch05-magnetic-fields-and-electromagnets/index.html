Magnetic Fields and Electromagnets

Field lines, current's field and the electromagnet.

Learning objectives

Students should be able to:

  • realise the attraction and repulsion between magnetic poles

  • examine the magnetic field around a magnet and describe the behaviour of a compass in it

  • represent magnetic field using field lines

  • realise the existence of a magnetic field due to moving charges or currents

  • examine field patterns around a long straight wire, a circular coil and a long solenoid

  • apply \(B = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}\) for a straight wire and \(B = \dfrac{\mu_0 N I}{l}\) inside a solenoid and solve problems

  • examine factors affecting the strength of an electromagnet
