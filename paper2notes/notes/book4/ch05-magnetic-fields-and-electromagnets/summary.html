Magnetic Fields and Electromagnets — summary

One-page revisit: formulae, field / circuit pictures, and the mistakes students most often make.

Formula sheet

\(B = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}\) — straight wire

\(B = \dfrac{\mu_0 N I}{l}\) — solenoid interior uniform

Field lines N→S outside; compass tangent; broken magnet → two magnets

Electromagnet ↑ with \(N, I,\) soft-iron core

Common mistakes

Field direction

Outside bar magnet N→S; wire circles via right-hand grip, not left-hand.

Solenoid vs wire

Wire B ∝1/r; solenoid B uniform ∝ N/l, not 1/r.
