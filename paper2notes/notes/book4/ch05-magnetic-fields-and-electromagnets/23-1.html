Fields of magnets and currents

ABar magnet and compass

Key idea

Magnetic field direction is the direction a free N pole would move (or compass N points). Lines N→S outside magnet, continuous through interior. Density ∝ strength. Isolated single pole not observed; broken magnet makes two new poles.

Key ideaDetail
NullTwo opposing fields can cancel to zero at a point between opposite currents.

Concept check

A bar magnet broken in half gives

Domains reform; each fragment has N and S. No monopole.

BField of a current

Key idea

Long straight wire: circles centred on wire, direction by right-hand grip (thumb = current, fingers = B). Magnitude \(B = \mu_0 I/(2\pi r)\). Circular coil centre: \(B \propto I/r\). Long solenoid: parallel inside, uniform: \(B = \mu_0 N I/l\). Electromagnet strength ↑ with N, I, soft iron core, tighter winding.

Key ideaDetail
μ0\(\mu_0 = 4\pi\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{T\,m\,A^{-1}}\).
SolenoidDoubling N/l doubles B; doubling I doubles B; steel core multiplies by relative permeability.

Concept check

B=μ0 N I /l =4πe-7*200*2/0.1=5.03e-3 T=5.0 mT.
