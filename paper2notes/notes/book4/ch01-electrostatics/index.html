Book 4 · Ch.1

Electrostatics

Charges, Coulomb's law and the electric field. Prior knowledge: only force and motion. Every field and circuit idea is taught from zero with a visual and a check.

Learning objectives

Students should be able to:

  • examine the evidence for two kinds of charges in nature

  • realise the attraction and repulsion between charges

  • state Coulomb's law \(F = \dfrac{Q_1 Q_2}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r^2}\)

  • interpret charging in terms of electron transfer

  • solve problems involving forces between point charges

  • describe the electric field around a point charge and between parallel charged plates

  • represent an electric field using field lines

  • explain how charges interact via an electric field

  • define electric field strength \(E\) at a point as the force per unit positive test charge

  • state \(E = \dfrac{Q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r^2}\) around a point charge and \(E = \dfrac{V}{d}\) between parallel plates and solve problems
