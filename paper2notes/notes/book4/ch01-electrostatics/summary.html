Ch.1 summary

Electrostatics — summary

One-page revisit: formulae, field / circuit pictures, and the mistakes students most often make.

Formula sheet

\(F = \dfrac{Q_1 Q_2}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r^2}\) — Coulomb (vector, superposition)

\(E = \dfrac{F}{q}\) — field = force per unit + test charge

\(E = \dfrac{Q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r^2}\) — point charge (radial)

\(E = \dfrac{V}{d}\) — uniform between plates

Common mistakes

Charge sign vs field arrow

Field arrow is force on + test. Electron feels opposite. Lines leaving + / entering −.

Coulomb vs field

F between two charges uses both Q's; E of one charge uses one Q and tells the force per unit test.
