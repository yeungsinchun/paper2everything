Ch.7 summary

Induction recap

Formula sheet

\(\Phi = B A \cos\theta\) — flux (Wb)

\(\bar\varepsilon = -N \dfrac{\Delta\Phi}{\Delta t}\) — Faraday (average)

Lenz: induced current opposes change

Generator: \(\varepsilon = N B A \omega \sin\omega t\); eddy currents braked by Lenz

Common mistakes

Sign of Lenz

Opposes change, not opposes B itself; if flux decreases, induced reinforces.

Flux vs rate

Zero flux can still give large ε if ΔΦ/Δt large; large Φ with no change gives zero.
