Ch.8 summary

AC, Transformers and Power Transmission — summary

One-page revisit: formulae, field / circuit pictures, and the mistakes students most often make.

Formula sheet

a.c. \(\,I = I_0\sin\omega t\); \(V_{\rm rms}=V_0/\sqrt2\) (sine)

\(P = V_{\rm rms} I_{\rm rms}\) resistive

Ideal transformer: \(\dfrac{V_P}{V_S}=\dfrac{N_P}{N_S},\ I_P V_P\approx I_S V_S\)

High \(V\) → lower \(I\) → \(I^2R\) loss ↓; grid step-up/step-down

Common mistakes

rms vs peak

Heating uses rms, not peak; √2 only for sine. d.c. doesn't transform.

Power vs voltage

High V reduces loss for same delivered power; doesn't increase delivered power by itself.
