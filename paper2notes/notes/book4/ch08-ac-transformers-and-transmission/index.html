AC, Transformers and Power Transmission

r.m.s., the transformer and the grid.

Learning objectives

Students should be able to:

  • distinguish between d.c. and a.c.

  • define r.m.s. of an a.c. as the steady d.c. which converts energy at the same rate in a pure resistance

  • relate the r.m.s. and peak values of an a.c. (\(V_{\mathrm{rms}} = V_0/\sqrt{2}\) for sinusoidal)

  • describe the structure of a simple transformer and how it works

  • relate voltage ratio to turn ratio by \(\dfrac{V_P}{V_S}=\dfrac{N_P}{N_S}\) and apply it

  • examine methods for improving the efficiency of a transformer

  • discuss advantages of high-voltage a.c. transmission

  • describe stages of stepping up and down in a grid system
