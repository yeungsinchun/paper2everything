Ch.3 summary

Series, Parallel and Internal Resistance — summary

One-page revisit: formulae, field / circuit pictures, and the mistakes students most often make.

Formula sheet

Series: \(R = R_1+R_2+\cdots,\ V = V_1+V_2,\ I\) same

Parallel: \(\dfrac{1}{R}=\dfrac{1}{R_1}+\dfrac{1}{R_2}+\cdots,\ I = I_1+I_2,\ V\) same

\(V = \varepsilon - I r\) — terminal vs e.m.f.

Ammeter \(\approx 0\ \Omega\) series; voltmeter \(\gg R\) parallel

Common mistakes

V split trap

Series V adds to ε only when counting internal r; parallel branches each see same V.

Meter loading

Low-R voltmeter shunts branch and reads low; high-R ammeter adds drop.
