Book 4 · Ch.3

Series, Parallel and Internal Resistance

Combining resistors, terminal voltage and meters.

Learning objectives

Students should be able to:

  • compare series and parallel circuits in terms of p.d. across components and current through them

  • derive \(R = R_1 + R_2 + \cdots\) for series and \(\dfrac{1}{R}=\dfrac{1}{R_1}+\dfrac{1}{R_2}+\cdots\) for parallel

  • measure \(I\), \(V\) and \(R\) in simple circuits

  • assign the potential of any earthed point as zero

  • compare the e.m.f. of a source and the terminal voltage across the source and relate the difference to internal resistance

  • explain the effects of resistance of ammeters and voltmeters on measurements

  • solve problems involving simple circuits
