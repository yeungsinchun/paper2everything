Section 21.3

Series and parallel combinations

AHow p.d. and current share

Key idea

Series: same I through each, V splits: \(V = V_1+V_2\), so \(R = R_1+R_2\) (more resistors always increase). Parallel: same V across each, I splits: \(I = I_1+I_2\), so \(1/R = 1/R_1+1/R_2\) (more branches always decrease). For two equal R in parallel, equivalent = R/2.

Key ideaDetail
Quick sums3 Ω+4 Ω series=7 Ω. 3 Ω//6 Ω =2 Ω ( \(1/3+1/6=1/2\) ).
Identical nn of R in parallel → R/n; in series → nR.

Quick check

Three identical resistors R: one in series with two in parallel. Equivalent is

Parallel pair R/2, plus series R → 1.5 R.

BEquivalent and potentials

Key idea

Assign earthed point 0 V, walk along current: drop I·R across resistor in direction of current, rise ε when crossing source −→+. Between any two nodes, V = sum of drops/rises on a path.

Key ideaDetail
EarthedIf point Y earthed (0 V), voltage at X = ±I·R or ε along path X→Y.

Quick check

In a divider 6 Ω (top) +3 Ω (bottom) to 9 V, voltmeter at midpoint reads

Current 1 A (9/9). Drop over top 6 Ω =6 V, so middle is 3 V above 0 (bottom). Or ratio 3/(6+3)=1/3 of 9.
