Book 4 · Ch.4

Power and Domestic Electricity

Heating, power rating, kWh and household safety. Prior knowledge: only force and motion. Every field and circuit idea is taught from zero with a visual and a check.

Learning objectives

Students should be able to:

  • examine the heating effect when a current passes through a conductor

  • apply \(P = V I\) to solve problems

  • determine the power rating of electrical appliances

  • use kilowatt-hour (kW h) as a unit of electrical energy and calculate running costs

  • understand household wiring and discuss safety aspects of domestic electricity

  • determine the operating current for electrical appliances

  • discuss the choice of power cables and fuses based on the power rating
