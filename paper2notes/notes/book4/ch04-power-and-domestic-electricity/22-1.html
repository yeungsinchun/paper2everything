Section 22.1

Heating and electrical power

Power is energy per second; heating is \(I^2 R\) whichever form you write it.

AP = VI and its forms

Key idea

\(P = V I = I^2 R = V^2/R\) (for pure resistor). Rating is at rated voltage; operating at lower V gives lower P (P ∝ V^2 at fixed R). Energy \(E = P t\); 1 kW h = 1 kW×1 h = 3.6 MJ, tariff × units.

Key ideaDetail
Cost2 kW heater 3 h → 6 kW h → tariff say $1.2/kW h → $7.2.
SafetyResistor has max power; exceeding burns.

Concept check

A 12 Ω resistor rated 3 W. Max safe voltage is

P=V^2/R → V=√(PR)=√36=6 V.

BkW h and appliance rating

Key idea

Power rating (e.g., 220 V 1 kW) is at rated voltage. Operating current at rating: \(I = P/V\). If both P and V given, \(R = V^2/P\). Carbon footprint/CO2 from kW h via grid factor.

Key ideaDetail
Example500 W at 220 V → I≈2.27 A, R≈96.8 Ω. At 110 V: P≈125 W (quarter).

Concept check

Stand-by 5 W for 30 days (24h) uses how many kW h?

0.005 kW×720 h=3.6 kW h.
