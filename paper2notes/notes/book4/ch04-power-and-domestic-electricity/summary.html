Formula sheet

Fuse: \(I_{\rm rating} > I_{\rm operating}\) but \(< I_{\rm cable\ max}\) on live

Common mistakes

P rating confusion

Operating at lower V gives lower P (∝ V²); rating is at rated V only.

Fuse location

Fuse must be on live, not neutral/earth; parallel not series for homes.
