Recent developments in nanotechnology

Worked example

Explain a catalyst use

  1. Nanoparticles can expose more surface area per unit volume.
  2. More surface sites can contact reactants.
  3. A suitable material can therefore catalyse a reaction effectively.

Quick check

Why can a nanoscale material be an effective catalyst?

B. More accessible sites can contact reactants; the effect depends on material and reaction.

CRisk and responsibility

Particles may enter the body by inhalation, ingestion or skin contact. Environmental release can spread through air and water. Small size alone does not prove either safety or harm.

Evaluate claims

Identify the material, exposure route, evidence limits and ways to reduce exposure.

Quick check

What conclusion is justified when evidence about one nanomaterial is incomplete?

C. Effects depend on the material and exposure. Do not generalize beyond available evidence.
