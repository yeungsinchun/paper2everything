Summary: Nanotechnology

Use the formula and definitions to connect particle scale with observable behaviour.

AFormula sheet

\[\lambda=\frac{h}{p}=\frac{h}{mv}\]

λ is de Broglie wavelength (m), h is Planck’s constant (J s), p is momentum (kg m/s), m is mass (kg), and v is speed (m/s).

\[\frac{A}{V}=\frac{6}{L}\quad\text{for a cube of side }L\]

A/V is surface area per volume (m⁻¹); L is side length (m).

\[2\pi r=n\lambda\quad\Longrightarrow\quad mvr=\frac{nh}{2\pi}\]

n is a positive integer; r is orbit radius.

BDefinitions

de Broglie wavelength
The wavelength associated with a particle of momentum p, λ = h/p.
Resolution
The ability to distinguish two nearby objects as separate.
Nanomaterial
A material with at least one dimension in the approximate 1–100 nm range.
Surface effect
A change in behaviour caused by the increased importance of surface atoms in small particles.
Quantum effect
A change in material behaviour caused by quantum rules becoming important at small scales.
Photocatalyst
A catalyst whose activity is initiated or enhanced by light.

CFast recall

QuestionRemember
When does diffraction show?When the wavelength is comparable to the gap or repeating spacing.
