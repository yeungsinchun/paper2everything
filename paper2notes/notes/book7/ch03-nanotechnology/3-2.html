Section 3.2

Observing at nanoscale

Resolving smaller structures requires finer resolution. Electron and scanning-probe methods reveal detail beyond visible-light microscopy.

In short

Nanoscale commonly covers dimensions from about 1 nm to 100 nm.

Resolution is the smallest separation at which two objects appear distinct.

Choose an instrument based on the scale, sample and signal.

AResolution and diffraction BElectron and probe microscopes CSelect an imaging method

AResolution and diffraction

Diffraction spreads the image of each object. When two patterns overlap too much, the objects cannot be distinguished.

Definition

Resolving power is the ability to distinguish two nearby objects. Better resolution means a smaller minimum resolvable separation.

Worked example

Apply the idea

An instrument resolves 0.25 μm separation but not 0.10 μm. Its resolution lies between those values; it cannot claim 0.10 μm.

Quick check

What happens when diffraction makes each point image wider?

A. Overlap increases, so the minimum resolvable separation becomes larger.

BElectron and probe microscopes

Electron microscopes use electron beams. Scanning-probe instruments move a sharp tip across a surface and measure its interaction.

Tool Signal Useful view
TEM Transmitted electrons Internal structure
SEM Scattered electrons Surface form
STM Tunnelling current Conducting surface
AFM Tip–surface force Conducting or insulating surface
Worked example

Apply the idea

For an insulating sample, use AFM to map surface topography. STM requires a tunnelling current between tip and sample.

Quick check

Which signal does an STM use?

A. STM records tunnelling current as the tip scans.

CSelect an imaging method

Magnification enlarges an image; resolution reveals separate details. The best instrument depends on what property needs measuring.

Selection rule

Name the target scale, whether it is internal or surface detail, and whether the sample conducts.

Worked example

Apply the idea

To map an insulating surface at nanoscale, choose AFM. STM would need measurable tunnelling current.

Quick check

Does greater magnification guarantee better resolution?

C. Enlarging a blurred image cannot reveal details the instrument did not resolve.
