Section 3.3

Materials in nanoscale

At nanoscale, exposed surface atoms and quantum behaviour can change the properties of a material.

In short

Nanomaterial has at least one dimension around 1–100 nm.

Smaller particles have a larger surface-area-to-volume ratio.

Atomic arrangement and quantum effects can change material behaviour.

AForms and atomic arrangement BSurface effects CQuantum effects and properties

AForms and atomic arrangement

Nanofilms are thin sheets, nanowires are long and narrow, and nanoparticles are small in all dimensions. Atomic arrangement also matters.

Form Structure Typical behaviour
Diamond Strong 3D network Hard; insulator
Graphite Sheets with weak links between them Soft; conducts along layers
Nanotube Rolled carbon network Strong; conducts along tube
C₆₀ Closed carbon cage Small fullerene molecule
Worked example

Apply the idea

Diamond and graphite contain carbon but have different bonding arrangements, producing different mechanical and electrical properties.

Quick check

Which carbon structure is a hollow tube?

A. A carbon nanotube is a rolled, hollow carbon structure.

BSurface effects

When a particle shrinks, a greater fraction of its atoms lie at the surface. Surface atoms can be more reactive than interior atoms.

\[\frac{A}{V}=\frac{6L^2}{L^3}=\frac{6}{L}\]

For a cube of side L, the surface-area-to-volume ratio rises as L falls.

Worked example

Apply the idea

A cube with side 2 nm has A/V = 6/(2 nm) = 3 nm⁻¹.

Quick check

What happens to surface area per volume when similar particles get smaller?

B. For a cube A/V = 6/L, so the ratio grows as L decreases.

CQuantum effects and properties

At small dimensions, electron wave behaviour and restricted energy states can change how matter absorbs light or conducts electricity.

Two causes

Surface effects expose more atoms. Quantum effects change electronic behaviour at small dimensions. Both can affect material properties.

Worked example

Apply the idea

A nanoscale material may differ from its bulk form because surface atoms matter more and quantum behaviour can change. The direction of change depends on the material.

Quick check

Which two effects can explain changed nanoscale properties?

A. Surface and quantum effects both matter; size does not change every material in the same way.
