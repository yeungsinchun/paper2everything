At sunset, the sun looks vertically compressed. This

Diamonds
Diamonds have an extraordinary high refractive index of 2.42. In other
words, their critical angles are small. If a diamond is properly cut, most
light entering its top is totally reflected and leaves from the top. Also,
coloured lights disperse when crossing any diamond-air boundary. As
a result, a good diamond sparkles when it is Lit.
