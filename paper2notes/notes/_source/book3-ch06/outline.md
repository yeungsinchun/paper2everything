## Student concepts

- **18.1: Refraction.** Light changes speed when it enters a different medium. It bends towards the normal on entering a slower medium.
- **18.2: Laws of refraction.** For a fixed pair of media, sin i / sin r is constant. The refractive index n = c/v, where c is the vacuum speed and v is speed in the medium.
- **18.3: Total internal reflection.** Total internal reflection needs travel from higher to lower refractive index and incidence above the critical angle; sin C = n₂/n₁.
- **18.4: More phenomena and applications.** Refraction explains apparent depth and mirages; total internal reflection guides light in optical fibres.
