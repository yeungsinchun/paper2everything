## Student concepts

- **13.1: Waves.** A wave carries energy as a disturbance; the medium oscillates about equilibrium.
- **13.2: Kinds of waves.** Mechanical waves need a medium. Electromagnetic waves can travel through a vacuum. Transverse motion is perpendicular to travel; longitudinal motion is parallel.
- **13.3: Terms for waves.** Amplitude is maximum displacement; wavelength is the spacing between successive points in phase. Frequency counts cycles per second; period is the time per cycle.
- **13.4: Particle motion and graphs.** Wave speed, frequency and wavelength obey v = fλ. A displacement–distance graph is a snapshot; a displacement–time graph follows one particle.
