## 24.3 + 22.3 Alternating current

- **Visual:** Fig. 21.2 d.c. flat vs a.c. sine; Fig. 21.4 r.m.s. heating equivalence.
- **Text:** a.c. reverses; r.m.s. = d.c. equivalent heating; Vrms = V0/√2 for sine; peak vs r.m.s. for power P = Vrms Irms.
- **Check:** HKDSE 2014/30, 2016/30 (rms unchanged by f), 2021/28. QB peak vs rms.

## 24.4 + 22.4 Transformers and transmission

- **Visual:** Fig. 21.6 transformer iron core, Fig. 21.8 ideal ratio, Fig. 21.10 grid steps, Fig. 21.12 losses table.
- **Text:** VP/VS = NP/NS ideal; power in ≈ power out for ideal (IP/IP vs IS); efficiency <100% due to copper/hysteresis/eddy/lamination; stepping up at high V reduces I^2R loss for long lines.
- **Check:** HKDSE 2016/31, 2017/30, 2019/29, 2020/29. QB 110V lamp transformer.
