## Student concepts

- **17.1: Light and vision.** We see an object when light from it enters the eye. A luminous object emits light; a non-luminous object reflects light.
- **17.2: Reflection.** The incident ray, reflected ray and normal lie in one plane. The angles are equal and measured from the normal.
- **17.3: Mirror image.** A plane mirror forms a virtual, upright image the same size as the object, at equal perpendicular distance behind the mirror.
- **17.4: More about reflection.** Diffuse reflection scatters rays from a rough surface; each local reflection still obeys the law of reflection.
