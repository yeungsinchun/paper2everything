## Student concepts

- **19.1: Basics of lenses.** A converging lens brings parallel rays to its principal focus. A diverging lens spreads parallel rays as if from a virtual focus.
- **19.2: Images formed by lenses.** A converging lens can form real or virtual images depending on object distance. A diverging lens forms an upright, diminished virtual image.
- **19.3: Graphical methods for lenses.** Use principal rays: parallel then through focus; through optical centre undeviated; through focus then parallel.
- **19.4: Lens formula.** For a thin lens, 1/f = 1/u + 1/v using the stated real-distance convention. Magnification m = image height/object height = v/u.
