e Terms of lenses
principal axis i F
1. The optical centre C is the centre of a lens.
2. The principal axis passes through C perpendicularly.
3. When light rays parallel to the principal axis pass through a convex
lens, they converge to a point on the axis. That point is called the
principal focus F or F’. Its distance from C is the focal length f. If the
lens is concave, the refracted light rays appear to diverge from the
principal focus instead.
4. The focal plane is the plane passing through the principal focus and
normal to the principal axis.
