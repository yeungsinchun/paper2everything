## Summary traps

- Fission per reaction > fusion per reaction but per kg fusion > fission (Summary mistake).
- Chain needs ≥1 neutron per fission to sustain; average not instantaneous.
- 1u as mass vs energy vs neutron mass confusion.
- Δm ×c² only the lost mass, not total mass.
