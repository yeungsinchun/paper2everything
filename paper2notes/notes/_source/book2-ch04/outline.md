## Ch.4 Newton II · connected bodies · Newton III

### A. Newton II · F=ma · free-body
- **Visual:** X(3 kg)–Y(5 kg) string pulled 24 N, tension animation; student trolley F vs a experiment.
- **Text:** F_net = ma (N = kg·m/s²). Free-body: weight mg down, normal N up, etc. Coupled bodies → same a, internal tension.
- **Check:** QB 2_ch04 MC (24 N → a=3 m/s², tension 15 N). HKDSE 2013/3 lift-counterweight.

### B. Newton III · mass vs weight
- **Visual:** action-reaction pairs (hand on block, Earth on mass).
- **Text:** Action = -Reaction (different bodies). Mass = amount of matter (kg). Weight = mg force (N) — varies with g.
- **Check:** QB 2_ch04 RQ static/kinetic friction explanation.
