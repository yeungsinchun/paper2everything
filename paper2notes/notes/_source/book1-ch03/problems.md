# Problem shortlist: Book 1 Ch.3 / syllabus section 3

Write original checks that target the misconceptions below. Keep scanned DSE questions in the section decks only. The textbook exercise is a source for coverage, not text to reproduce.

## 3.1 Change of state
- Distinguish invisible water vapour from visible liquid droplets; check the cooling effect of evaporation.
- Contrast evaporation at a surface with boiling throughout a liquid; avoid claiming evaporation only happens at boiling point.
- Explain why higher temperature, larger exposed area, lower humidity and airflow increase net evaporation.
- Track energy released in condensation/freezing and absorbed in vaporization/melting.
- Worked example: infer which factors speed drying; concept check on what cools and what warms.

## 3.2 Latent heat
- Interpret a flat section of a cooling/heating curve: energy continues to transfer, temperature and average molecular KE stay constant, and phase proportions change.
- Relate latent heat to molecular potential energy and bond changes.
- Read phase and transition points from a heating or cooling graph.
- Worked example: partition a heating process into temperature-change and phase-change stages.

## 3.3 Specific latent heat
- Use \(E=m\ell\), with mass referring only to the portion that changes state; retain SI units.
- Calculate energy or mass from fusion/vaporization data and compare measured versus standard values.
- Assess experiment controls: surrounding heat transfer, heater immersion, insulation, steady boiling, and mass lost as vapour.
- Explain evaporative cooling and why sweat can carry substantial energy away.
- Worked example: compute energy and heating time across several state/temperature stages.
