## 3.2 Latent heat (printed pp.98–106)
- Latent heat changes internal energy while temperature stays constant during a phase change.
- Molecular KE tracks temperature; latent heat changes molecular PE as bonds form or break.
- Read heating/cooling curves and identify mixed-phase plateaus.
- Visuals: annotated heating/cooling curve; particle-energy bars across phases.
- Checks and worked examples: plateau interpretation, energy bookkeeping, staged ice-to-steam calculation.

## 3.3 Specific latent heat (printed pp.107–123)

- Evaporative cooling; staged energy calculations across temperature and state changes.
- Visuals: labelled calorimetry apparatus, evaporation mass/temperature graph, state-change energy path.
- Worked examples: electrical heating data, cooling by evaporation, staged phase-change calculation.
