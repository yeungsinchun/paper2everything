## 22.1 Electrical power and heating

- **Visual:** Fig. 17.2 I^2R glow; Fig. 17.4 P=VI rating table.
- **Text:** Heating ∝ I^2 R; P = VI = I^2 R = V^2/R; power rating at rated voltage; choosing R for dissipation limit.
- **Check:** HKDSE 2018/24 (power limit). QB LQ heater.

## 22.2 Domestic electricity and safety

- **Visual:** Fig. 17.8 household ring (live/neutral/earth), Fig. 17.6 kWh meter, Fig. 17.10 fuse table.
- **Text:** kWh = energy; cost = power × time × tariff; parallel sockets; earth for safety; fuse rating > operating I but < cable max; MCB/RCCB.
- **Check:** HKDSE 2012/33 (fuse blows), 2013/33 (1kW appliance), 2016/28 (stand-by CO2). QB wiring fault.
