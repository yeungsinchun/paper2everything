## Ch.10 Gravitation

### A. Universal gravitation
- **Visual:** Earth-Sun orbit, GPS satellite, field strength vs r.
- **Text:** F=GMm/r² (G=6.67×10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg²). At surface g=GM/R². Gravitational field strength = F/m.
- **Check:** QB 2_ch10 MC half distance → 129 days (Kepler T²∝r³), SQ Earth angular speed.

### B. Orbits and field strength
- **Visual:** circular orbit v=√(GM/r), variation with height.
- **Text:** Orbit: centripetal=GMm/r² → v=√(GM/r), higher → slower. Field weakens as 1/r².
- **Check:** QB 2_ch10 LQ GPS satellite signals.
