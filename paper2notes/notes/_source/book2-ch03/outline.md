### B. Inertia, Newton I, friction
- **Visual:** block on rough surface, static vs kinetic friction animation.
- **Text:** Inertia = resistance to change in motion (∝ mass). Newton I: no net force → at rest or uniform motion. Friction opposes motion/tendency, ≤ μN.
- **Check:** QB 2_ch03 SQ free-body, Road Users' Code stopping distance (thinking + braking).
