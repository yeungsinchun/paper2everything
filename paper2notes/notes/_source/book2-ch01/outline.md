## Ch.1 Position and displacement

### A. Position, distance vs displacement
- **Visual:** Fig. displacement 2-· trench mouse A→B→C (500 m legs), vector arrow from A to C, Peter O→P circular path comparison.
- **Text:** Distance = path length (scalar, m). Displacement = change in position (vector, m, direction). Same distance can give different displacement (trench). Magnitude ≤ distance.
- **Check:** QB PHY1 2 01 1 01 (mouse average speed vs velocity). HKDSE 2023/3 (position vs distance).

### B. Scalars and vectors · adding vectors
- **Visual:** tip-to-tail, Shirley/Simon resultant animation, 2-D arrow grid.
- **Text:** Scalar: magnitude only (distance, speed, mass). Vector: magnitude+direction (displacement, velocity, force). Add by parallelogram/triangle; resultant is single vector.
- **Check:** DSE classification 05 Motion MC (vector sum). Checkpoint: is current scalar or vector? (no, has direction).
