## Ch.2 Velocity and acceleration · UAM

### A. Speed and velocity · instantaneous vs average
- **Visual:** s-t graph slope = velocity, v-t area = displacement, a-t graph.
- **Text:** Average speed = distance/time. Average velocity = displacement/time. Instantaneous = limit Δ→0 (tangent slope). Units m/s, same magnitude for straight line only.
- **Check:** QB 2_ch02 MC (ball fall 3 m/s to -2 m/s, Δv over 0.2 s).

### B. Acceleration and UAM equations
- **Visual:** v=u+at as v-t straight line, s=ut+½at² as parabola, ticker tape dots animation.
- **Text:** a = dv/dt (m s⁻²). UAM: v=u+at, s=(u+v)t/2, s=ut+½at², v²=u²+2as. Use one equation per unknown, check sign for direction.
- **Check:** HKDSE 2016/3 (UAM on slope ratio 1:3 speed). Fire-jump LQ (2.5 s fall, 3 m cushion).

### C. Free-fall and g
- **Visual:** free-fall experiment, vertical s-t/v-t/a-t with g ≈9.81 m/s².
- **Text:** Without air, all bodies fall with same g downward. With air, terminal. g measured via light gate / ticker; graph v vs t slope = g.
- **Check:** Textbook free-fall estimate, QB 2_ch02 RQ hang-time.
