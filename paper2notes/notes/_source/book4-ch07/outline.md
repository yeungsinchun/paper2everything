## 24.1 Flux, Lenz and Faraday

- **Visual:** Fig. 20.2 flux geometric cosθ; Fig. 20.4 Lenz opposition animation (magnet in/out); Fig. 20.6 Faraday N·ΔΦ/Δt table.
- **Text:** Φ = B A cosθ (Wb); B = flux density; Faraday ε = -N ΔΦ/Δt (average); Lenz direction opposes change; search coil ∫ε dt = Δ(NΦ).
- **Check:** HKDSE 2014/27 (coil from wire), 2013/29 (rotating rod). QB 180° rotation.

## 24.2 Generators and eddy currents

- **Visual:** Fig. 20.10 generator coils: a.c. slip rings vs d.c. commutator; Fig. 20.8 search coil; Fig. 20.12 eddy braked magnet tube.
- **Text:** Rotating coil in B → sinusoidal e.m.f.; frequency ∝ rotation speed; d.c. generator steadier with commutator; eddy currents dissipate, damp motion, used in braking, unwanted in cores (laminated).
- **Check:** HKDSE 2012/31 (frame duration), 2018/29 (magnet tube), 2022/28 (generator). QB structure compare.
