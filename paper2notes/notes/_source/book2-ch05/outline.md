## Ch.5 Moment · equilibrium · centre of gravity

### A. Moment and torque
- **Visual:** seesaw P Q R S, board ABCD 0.3 m pivoted at B with 25 N at A etc., moment = F·d⊥ animation.
- **Text:** Moment = force × perpendicular distance from pivot (N·m). Couple = two equal opposite forces.
- **Check:** QB 2_ch05 MC seesaw swap P↔Q, R↔S still balanced → masses P=Q etc.

### B. Equilibrium and centre of gravity
- **Visual:** conditions ΣF=0, ΣM=0; C.G. plumb-line experiment.
- **Text:** Rigid body equilibrium: net force zero + net moment about any pivot zero. C.G. = point where weight acts.
- **Check:** QB 2_ch05 LQ square board 25 N/50 N equilibrium.
