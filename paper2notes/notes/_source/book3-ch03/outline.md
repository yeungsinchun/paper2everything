## Student concepts

- **15.1: Superposition.** When waves overlap, the resultant displacement is the algebraic sum of their individual displacements.
- **15.2: Stationary waves.** Two similar waves travelling in opposite directions form nodes with zero displacement and antinodes with maximum displacement.
- **15.3: Interference.** Coherent sources have the same frequency and a constant phase difference. Path difference determines constructive or destructive interference.
