## Ch.7 Momentum · impulse · collisions

### A. Momentum p = mv and impulse
- **Visual:** hose water 20 cm/s 100 cm² → force on wall animation (Δp/Δt), trolley collision bar.
- **Text:** p = mv (kg·m/s, vector). F_net = Δp/Δt (Newton II form). Impulse = F·Δt = Δp. Longer Δt → smaller F for same Δp (safety).
- **Check:** QB 2_ch07 MC hose force 4 N. RQ safety in sports impulse.

### B. Conservation and collisions
- **Visual:** A(5.5 kg, 2 m/s) head-on B(7 kg, 1.5 m/s) stick together → v=0.28 m/s.
- **Text:** Isolated system: total p before = after (from Newton III). Elastic: KE conserved too; inelastic: KE loss.
- **Check:** QB 2_ch07 LQ collision, SQ throw up with air drag momentum direction.
