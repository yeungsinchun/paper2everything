## Ch.6 Work · energy · power

### A. Work W = Fs cosθ
- **Visual:** chair 200 kg up 20° cable, diver 60 kg → 3 m drop animation with force projection.
- **Text:** Work = energy transfer by force along displacement. W = Fs cosθ (J). Positive if θ<90°, negative if >90°.
- **Check:** QB 2_ch06 SQ chair power, MC diver depth 2.18 m.

### B. PE = mgh, KE = ½mv², conservation
- **Visual:** PE→KE bar chart along slope, pumped storage.
- **Text:** PE = mgh (relative to reference). KE = ½mv² derived from W=Fs and v²=u²+2as. Closed system: E_total constant (with loss as note).
- **Check:** QB 2_ch06 LQ worker 75 kg lifted 3.5 m → ΔPE=2572 J.

### C. Power P = W/t
- **Visual:** P = Fv when constant speed.
- **Text:** Power = rate of energy transfer (W = J/s). For uniform: P=F·v.
- **Check:** Hose power / motor raise.
