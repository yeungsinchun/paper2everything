## Student concepts

- **16.1: Light.** Light is an electromagnetic transverse wave. It travels through vacuum and transfers energy without transporting matter.
- **16.2: Sound.** Sound is a longitudinal mechanical wave. Compressions and rarefactions travel through a medium; pitch relates to frequency and loudness to amplitude.
- **16.3: Comparing light and sound.** Light and sound both transfer energy as waves, but only sound requires a material medium. Their speeds and particle motions differ.
