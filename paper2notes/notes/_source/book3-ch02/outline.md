## Student concepts

- **14.1: Illustrating waves.** Wavefronts join points in the same phase; rays show the direction of travel and meet wavefronts at right angles.
- **14.2: Reflection.** At a boundary, the reflected wave obeys angle of incidence = angle of reflection, measured from the normal. Reflection can reverse a pulse at a fixed end.
- **14.3: Refraction.** A wave changes direction when its speed changes across a boundary. Frequency stays fixed; wavelength changes because v = fλ.
- **14.4: Diffraction.** Waves spread after passing an opening or edge. Spreading is strongest when the gap is comparable to the wavelength.
