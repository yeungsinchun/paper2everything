## 4.1 Gas pressure (printed pp.134–142)
- Pressure is normal force per area; gas pressure comes from molecule collisions with container walls.
- Pressure depends on collision frequency and impulse, affected by number density, volume and temperature.
- Atmospheric pressure; pressure imbalance; force balance on a piston.
- Visuals: particle collisions on walls; pressure-force balance; piston force diagram.

## 4.2 Gas laws (printed pp.143–161)
- Boyle's law: fixed temperature and amount, \(pV=\mathrm{constant}\).
- Pressure law: fixed volume and amount, \(p/T=\mathrm{constant}\), with Kelvin temperature.
- Charles' law: fixed pressure and amount, \(V/T=\mathrm{constant}\), with Kelvin temperature.
- Combined gas equation and ideal gas law \(pV=nRT\); Avogadro constant and mole.
- Apply constraints before choosing a gas relation; convert Celsius to kelvins.
- Visuals: original law graphs, Kelvin scale, paired-state reasoning diagrams.
