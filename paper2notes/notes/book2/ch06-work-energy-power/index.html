Work, energy and power

Energy moves; it is never lost.

Goals

Students should be able to:

  • interpret mechanical work as energy transfer, define \( W = Fs\cos\theta \) and solve

  • state PE as energy due to position, derive \( \text{PE}=mgh \)

  • state KE as energy due to motion, derive \( \text{KE}=\tfrac12 mv^2 \)

  • state conservation of energy in a closed system, discuss PE–KE inter-conversion with loss

  • define power as rate of energy transfer \( P = W/t \) and apply

AWork \( W=Fs\cos\theta \)

Work

\( W = Fs\cos\theta \) (J = N·m). \( \theta \) is between \( \vec F \) and \( \vec s \). Positive if force helps motion, negative if it opposes. Net work = change in kinetic energy.

Case\( \theta \)\( W \)
Force along \( s \)\( 0^\circ \)\( +Fs \)
Force ⊥ \( s \) (e.g. normal)\( 90^\circ \)0
Force opposite \( s \)\( 180^\circ \)\( -Fs \)

Quick check

Chair 200 kg at constant 5 m s\(^{-1}\) up 20°, drag 96 N. Power of motor?

Need \( F_{\text{cable}} = mg\sin20^\circ + 96 \approx 200·9.81·0.342+96≈767\ \text{N} \). \( P=Fv≈3835\ \text{W} \). At constant v the work goes into PE and heat, not KE.

BEnergy and conservation

Energies

\[ \text{PE}=mgh \quad (\text{relative}) \qquad \text{KE}=\tfrac12 mv^2 \qquad E_{\text{mech}}=\text{PE}+\text{KE} \] Closed isolated system: \( E_{\text{total}} \) constant — PE↔KE with some loss to heat.

SituationUse
Drop from rest, no drag\( mgh = \tfrac12 mv^2 \)
Diver 60 kg, 3 m drop into water with 1500 N drag to depth \( d \)\( mg(3+d)=F_{\text{water}} d \) → \( d≈1.18\ \text{m} \)
Worker 75 kg lifted 3.5 m at 0.2 m s\(^{-1}\)\( \Delta\text{PE}=mgh=2572\ \text{J} \) uniform → no ΔKE

Quick check

Diver 60 kg from 3 m, water 1500 N. How deep does he go (no air drag)?

Energy over total fall \( 3+d \): \( mg(3+d)=1500 d \) → \( 588(3+d)=1500 d \) → \( 1764=912 d \) → \( d≈1.94 \) m.

CPower \( P=W/t=Fv \)

Quick check

A motor raises the chair at 5 m s\(^{-1}\) with tension ~767 N. Its power?

\( P=Fv≈767×5≈3835\ \text{W} \). Power is the rate, not the total work.

DPumped storage

Quick check

Why is “energy lost” not lost?

It became heat/sound, not mechanical — total \( E \) same, useful \( E \) less. That is “with consideration of energy loss” in the syllabus.

ERoller track energy

Quick check

PE has an absolute zero independent of reference.

False — \( mgh \) is relative; only differences matter.

QDSE practice

2012 MC Q9

A motor raises a 0.5 kg object vertically by 2.5 m in 1.5 s at constant speed. Estimate its output power using g = 9.8 m s⁻².

P = mgh/t = 0.5 × 9.8 × 2.5 / 1.5 ≈ 8.2 W.
