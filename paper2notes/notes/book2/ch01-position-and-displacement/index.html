Position and displacement

Track the path, compare it with the straight-line change in position, then read motion from a graph.

Learning objectives

Students should be able to:

  • describe the change of position of objects in terms of distance and displacement

  • present information on displacement-time graphs for moving objects

  • distinguish between scalar and vector quantities

  • use scalars and vectors to represent physical quantities

ADistance vs displacement

Definition

Distance is the length of the path (scalar, no direction). Displacement \( \Delta\vec s = \vec s_{\text{final}} - \vec s_{\text{initial}} \) is the vector from start to finish. Same distance can give different displacements.

DistanceDisplacement
What it isPath lengthChange in position (vector)
Sign\( \ge 0 \) alwaysSign is direction (e.g. + east)
Relation\( |\vec s| \le \text{distance} \); equal only for straight without reversal

Concept check

The mouse’s average speed is \( 1.6\ \text{m s}^{-1} \). Which statement is correct about the trip A→B→C (500 m + 500 m)?

Distance 1000 m, time \( 1000/1.6 = 625\ \text{s} \). Displacement is the diagonal \( \sqrt{500^2+500^2}=707\ \text{m} \). Velocity \( 707/625 =1.13\ \text{m s}^{-1} \) along AC, smaller than speed. Displacement is a vector.

BScalars, vectors and adding them

Scalar (magnitude only)Vector (magnitude + direction)

A vector is drawn as an arrow: length = magnitude, direction = direction. Add by putting tail to tip.

Concept check

Peter’s average speed equals the magnitude of his average velocity for the O→P→O loop.

False — loop displacement zero, so average velocity zero, but speed is distance/time >0. Scalar vs vector.

Shirley and Simon’s resultant is found by tip-to-tail.

True — vectors add head-to-tail; the order does not matter.

2015 MC Q4 · Section 05 Motion

A particle travels at 2.0 m s⁻¹ due east for 1.5 s and then at 4.0 m s⁻¹ due north for 1.0 s. Find the magnitude of its average velocity for the whole journey.

The displacements are 3.0 m east and 4.0 m north, so the resultant displacement is √(3.0² + 4.0²) = 5.0 m. Average velocity = 5.0 m / 2.5 s = 2.0 m s⁻¹.
