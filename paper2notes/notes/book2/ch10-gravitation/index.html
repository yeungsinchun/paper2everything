Book 2 · Ch.10

Gravitation

GMm/r², field and orbits. Every mass pulls every other with \( 1/r^2 \). That single law gives weight, field strength, and how fast a satellite must circle.

Learning objectives

Students should be able to:

  • state Newton’s law of universal gravitation \( F = GMm/r^2 \)

  • define gravitational field strength as force per unit mass, determine \( g \) above a planet

  • determine velocity of object in circular orbit, solve problems involving gravitation

AUniversal gravitation — \( F=GMm/r^2 \)

Newton’s law

\[ F = \frac{GMm}{r^2} \qquad G = 6.67\times10^{-11}\ \text{N m}^2\text{kg}^{-2} \] \( r \) is centre-to-centre distance. \( F \) is the same on both bodies (Newton III).

Concept check

Earth–Sun distance became half (circular orbit). New year?

Gravity = centripetal: \( GMm/r^2 = m\omega^2 r \), \( \omega=2\pi/T \), so \( T^2 = 4\pi^2 r^3/(GM) \). Halve \( r \) → \( T^2 \) × \( 1/8 \) → \( T \) × \( 1/(2\sqrt2) \) ≈0.3535 → 129 d.

BOrbits and field strength

Orbit condition

\[ \frac{GMm}{r^2} = \frac{mv^2}{r} \quad\Rightarrow\quad v = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}} \qquad \omega = \frac{v}{r} \] Measured Earth orbit (\( r=1.496×10^{11} \) m, \( T=365.3 \) d) estimates Earth’s \( M_{\text{sun}} \) etc.

Concept check

Estimate Earth’s angular speed around Sun (take \( T=365.3 \) d).

\( \omega =2\pi/T =2\pi/(365.3×86400)≈1.99×10^{-7}\ \text{rad s}^{-1} \). Then \( g_{\text{field}} \) vs height or \( M \) from \( v^2 r = GM \).
