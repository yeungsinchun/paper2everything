Force and Motion

From how to describe motion to why it happens — displacement, velocity, acceleration, forces, energy, momentum, projectile, circular motion and gravity. Assume only that an object moves and a force can change its motion. Each chapter has visuals first, concise text, and topic-matched past-paper practice in the section.

Ch.1 Position and displacement Distance vs displacement · scalars and vectors · adding vectors tip-to-tail · resultant and the mouse trench Ch.2 Velocity, acceleration and uniformly accelerated motion Speed vs velocity · \( s\!-\!t \), \( v\!-\!t \), \( a\!-\!t \) · \( v = u + at \) etc. · free-fall and \( g \) · ticker tape Ch.3 Forces and Newton’s first law Force as vector · net force · resolution \( F_x = F\cos\theta \) · inertia · friction · stopping distance Ch.4 Newton’s second and third laws \( F_{\text{net}} = ma \) · free-body diagrams · connected bodies and tension · mass vs weight · action–reaction Ch.5 Moment, equilibrium and centre of gravity Moment \( = F d_\perp \) · torque and couple · \( \sum F = 0,\ \sum M = 0 \) · finding the centre of gravity Ch.6 Work, energy and power \( W = Fs\cos\theta \) · \( \text{PE}=mgh \), \( \text{KE}=\tfrac12 mv^2 \) · conservation · \( P = W/t = Fv \) Ch.7 Momentum and collisions \( p=mv \) · impulse \( F\Delta t = \Delta p \) · \( F = \Delta p/\Delta t \) · conservation · elastic vs inelastic Ch.8 Projectile motion Parabolic path · horizontal \( v_h \) constant · vertical \( g \) · same time \( t \) · bomber and baseball Ch.9 Uniform circular motion \( \omega = \Delta\theta/\Delta t \), \( v = \omega r \) · \( a = v^2/r = \omega^2 r \) · centripetal force · G-value Ch.10 Gravitation \( F = GMm/r^2 \) · field strength \( g = GM/r^2 \) · orbital speed \( v = \sqrt{GM/r} \) · Kepler \( T^2 \propto r^3 \)
