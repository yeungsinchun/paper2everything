Momentum and collisions

Momentum is conserved when no net external force acts.

Goals

Students should be able to:

  • realise momentum as quantity of motion \( p=mv \)

  • understand net force for a time results in change in momentum, interpret \( F = \Delta p/\Delta t \)

  • state conservation of momentum and relate to Newton’s Third Law

  • distinguish elastic and inelastic collisions, solve problems in one dimension

AMomentum \( p=mv \) and impulse

Impulse

\( \vec p = m\vec v \) (vector). \( \vec F_{\text{net}} = \dfrac{\Delta\vec p}{\Delta t} \) (Newton II form). Impulse \( = \vec F\Delta t = \Delta\vec p \). For the same \( \Delta p \), longer \( \Delta t \) means smaller \( F \) — helmets, airbags, bending knees.

Quick check

Water \( \rho=1000\ \text{kg m}^{-3} \) at \( 20\ \text{cm s}^{-1} \) through \( 100\ \text{cm}^2 \) hits wall and stops normal to wall. Force on wall?

\( \dot m = \rho A v =1000×0.01×0.20=2\ \text{kg s}^{-1} \). \( F = \dot m \Delta v =2×0.20=0.4\ \text{N} \). Force = mass rate × change in velocity.

BConservation and collisions

Conservation

Isolated system (no external net force or little over short impulse): \( \sum \vec p_{\text{before}} = \sum \vec p_{\text{after}} \). Follows from Newton III — internal forces cancel. Ball thrown up with air drag returns with less momentum magnitude because drag was external.

In a head-on stick collision of opposite directions, the heavier slower trolley can reverse the lighter faster one.

True if its momentum magnitude is larger — sign matters.

Air resistance is an internal force for the ball.

False — air is external to the ball, so it removes momentum from the ball system.

CSafety: time spreads the force

Quick check

Which reduces injury for same fall \( \Delta p \)?

B — \( F=\Delta p/\Delta t \); spread the impulse.

DThrow up with air drag

Quick check

Why is return momentum smaller?

Air was an external impulse on the ball; it carried momentum away.

QDSE practice

2014 MC Q7

A 2 kg sphere moving at 6 m s⁻¹ collides head-on with a stationary 1 kg sphere. What total momentum must their final velocities preserve?

The total initial momentum is 2 × 6 = 12 kg m s⁻¹ in the first sphere’s original direction.
