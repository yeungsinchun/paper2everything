Chapter summary

A cosmic journey

The relationships and distinctions to recall before solving problems.

Formula sheet

1 AU = 1.496 × 10¹¹ m; 1 ly = 9.461 × 10¹⁵ m; 1 pc = 3.26 ly.

Check units and conditions before substituting values.

Definitions

  • AU — mean Earth–Sun distance.
  • Light year — distance light travels in a year.
  • Parsec — distance corresponding to one arcsecond of parallax.
  • Planet — non-luminous body orbiting a star.
  • Satellite — body orbiting a planet.
  • Nebula — interstellar cloud of gas and dust.
  • Star cluster — stars held together by gravity.
  • Galaxy — stars, gas and dust bound by gravity.
  • Void — large region containing few galaxies.

Key ideas

  • 1.1 · Distances in the universe — Scale and astronomical distance units.
  • 1.2 · Celestial bodies — Planets, stars and the cosmic web.
Common mistakes

A light year is distance, not time. Planets reflect starlight; stars make their own.
