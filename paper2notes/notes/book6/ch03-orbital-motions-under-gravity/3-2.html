AGravity supplies centripetal force

Key idea

For a circular orbit GMm/r² = mv²/r, giving v = √(GM/r). The orbiting body’s mass cancels.

A larger radius means lower orbital speed and a longer period.

Quick check

Does the satellite mass set its circular speed?

No. It cancels from the force equation.

Worked example: low orbit speed

Satellite 630 km above Earth

  1. Use r = 6.37 × 10⁶ + 0.63 × 10⁶ = 7.00 × 10⁶ m.
  2. v = √(GM/r) = √[(3.986 × 10¹⁴)/(7.00 × 10⁶)] ≈ 7.55 × 10³ m s⁻¹.

The orbital speed is about 7.55 km s⁻¹.

BOrbital period and central mass

Key idea

Combining v = 2πr/T with gravity gives T² = 4π²r³/(GM). A moon’s orbit can reveal its planet’s mass.

Rearrange to M = 4π²r³/(GT²); use metres, seconds and SI units for G.

Quick check

Which satellite has a longer period around the same planet?

The outer satellite has the longer period.
