APotential and mechanical energy

Key idea

With zero potential energy at infinity, U = −GMm/r. Without drag or thrust, K + U stays constant along an orbit.

For a circular orbit, K = GMm/(2r) and E = U/2 = −K. This relation does not hold at every point of an ellipse.

Quick check

As r grows, U approaches?

It approaches zero from below.

BEscape speed

Key idea

Escape speed makes total energy at launch at least zero: vesc = √(2GM/R) = √2 vcircular. It does not depend on launch mass.

Earth’s escape speed is about 11.2 km s⁻¹.

Quick check

If launch mass doubles, does escape speed change?

No. Mass cancels from the energy balance.

Worked example: energy needed to raise an orbit

Compare circular-orbit energies

A satellite in a circular orbit has total energy E = −GMm/(2r). Raising r makes E less negative.

  1. For fixed mass, doubling r changes E to E/2.
  2. The increase is positive work supplied to the satellite system.

A higher circular orbit has greater total energy but lower speed.
