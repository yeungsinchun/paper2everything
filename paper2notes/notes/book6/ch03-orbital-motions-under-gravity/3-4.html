Apparent weightlessness

Kepler’s laws connect orbital shape, speed, gravity and energy.

Remember

Core ideaGravity accelerates an orbiting spacecraft and its occupants together. With no supporting contact force, an astronaut feels weightless although gravity remains strong.

FormulaApparent weight is the support force. A falling lift approximates the same shared free fall.

AvoidSeparate the model’s appearance from the physical cause.

Key idea

Gravity accelerates an orbiting spacecraft and its occupants together. With no supporting contact force, an astronaut feels weightless although gravity remains strong.

Apparent weight is the support force. A falling lift approximates the same shared free fall.

Quick check

Is gravity zero in low Earth orbit?

No. Gravity supplies the centripetal acceleration.
