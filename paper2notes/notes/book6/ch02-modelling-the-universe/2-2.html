AGeocentric spheres

Key idea

Early Greek models placed Earth at the centre. Eudoxus represented celestial motion with nested rotating spheres.

The geocentric model made Earth central; it was a model, not direct evidence that Earth was stationary.

Quick check

In a geocentric model, what is central?

Earth is central by definition.

BEpicycles and deferents

Key idea

Ptolemy placed a planet on a small circle called an epicycle, whose centre moved on a larger deferent around Earth. Their combined motion reproduced loops.

The model also accounted for changes in apparent brightness. Mercury and Venus stayed near the Sun’s direction.

Quick check

What is the smaller circle called?

The planet moves on the epicycle; its centre follows the deferent.
