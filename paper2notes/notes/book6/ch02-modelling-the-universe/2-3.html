AThe heliocentric model

Key idea

Copernicus treated Earth as a moving planet around the Sun. Earth’s rotation explains daily motion; changing orbital positions explain retrograde motion.

For an inner planet, sin θmax = r/R, where r and R are its orbital radius and Earth’s radius.

Quick check

For Mercury at 0.39 AU, what is sin θmax?

The ratio is r/R = 0.39/1 = 0.39.

Worked example: maximum elongation

Mercury’s apparent limit

  1. Mercury’s orbital radius is 0.39 AU; Earth’s is 1.00 AU.
  2. sin θ = 0.39/1.00, so θ = sin⁻¹(0.39) ≈ 23°.

Mercury stays within about 23° of the Sun.

BEvidence from telescopes

Key idea

Jupiter’s moons showed that not every body orbits Earth. Venus’s full phase cycle supported a Sun-centred orbit. Kepler later fitted Tycho’s precise observations with ellipses.

Moon craters and sunspots challenged the idea that celestial objects were perfect.

Quick check

Which observation contradicts Ptolemy’s Venus arrangement?

Full Venus phases require Venus to pass around the far side of the Sun.
