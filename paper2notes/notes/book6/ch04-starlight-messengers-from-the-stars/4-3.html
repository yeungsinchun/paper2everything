ADoppler shift

Key idea

For speeds much below c, Δλ/λ ≈ vr/c. Longer wavelength means recession; shorter wavelength means approach.

Only line-of-sight motion contributes to radial velocity.

Quick check

A line shifts to longer wavelength. The star is?

It is receding: the line is red-shifted.

Worked example: a red-shifted line

Wavelength shift to speed

A spectral line moves from 500 nm to 501 nm. Assume the source speed is much smaller than c.

  1. Δλ/λ = 1/500 = 0.002.
  2. vr = c(Δλ/λ) = 3.00 × 10⁸ × 0.002 = 6.0 × 10⁵ m s⁻¹.

The source recedes at about 600 km s⁻¹.

BWobble, dark matter and expansion

Key idea

Periodic spectral shifts reveal a star’s motion around a shared centre and can expose orbiting planets. Flat galaxy rotation curves imply extra gravitating matter.

For distant galaxies, Hubble’s law gives v = H₀d. Nearby bound galaxies need not follow it.

Quick check

By Hubble’s law, which distant galaxy recedes faster?

The farther galaxy, because v is proportional to d.
