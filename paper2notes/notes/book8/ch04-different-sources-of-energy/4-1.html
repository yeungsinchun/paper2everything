Section 4.1

Electricity and energy

Where mains electricity comes from, and which energy sources will last.

What to remember

Generatorturns the kinetic energy of a spinning turbine into electrical energy by electromagnetic induction

Turbinea large wheel turned by steam, water or air; the energy source makes that flow

Renewablereplaced in a short time: solar, wind, hydroelectric, geothermal, biomass

Non-renewableused up far faster than it forms: coal, oil, natural gas, nuclear fuel

AGenerating electricity BRenewable or not

AFrom energy source to the mains

  • A generator uses electromagnetic induction to turn mechanical energy into electrical energy.
  • It is driven by a turbine: a large wheel of blades turned by a flow of steam, water or air.
  • The energy source creates that flow: burning fuel or nuclear fission boils water into steam; a dam releases falling water; the wind is moving air.

Quick check

True or false?

Mains electricity is supplied by very large batteries.

Mains electricity comes from generators in power stations.

A turbine turns the kinetic energy of steam directly into electrical energy.

The turbine only gives rotational kinetic energy; the generator it drives makes the electrical energy.

BRenewable and non-renewable sources

Definition

A renewable energy source is replaced naturally in a short time. A non-renewable source is used up much faster than it forms, so it can run out.

SourceTypeWhere its energy comes from
SolarRenewableThe Sun directly
WindRenewableThe Sun heats the Earth unevenly; pressure differences make wind
HydroelectricRenewableThe Sun evaporates water, which falls as rain on high ground
GeothermalRenewableHeat inside the Earth, much of it from radioactive decay in rocks
BiomassRenewablePlants store sunlight as chemical energy (for example ethanol from corn)
Coal, oil, natural gasNon-renewableRemains of plants and animals buried about 100 million years ago
Nuclear (uranium)Non-renewableNuclear energy of uranium mined from the ground

Quick check

Which energy source is non-renewable?

Uranium is mined and cannot be replaced. Wind and rain are renewed by the Sun every day, and the Earth's internal heat lasts for billions of years.
