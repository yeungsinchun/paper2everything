ACore model: Graphical methods for lenses

Key idea

Use principal rays: parallel then through focus; through optical centre undeviated; through focus then parallel.

Parallel ray → focus · optical-centre ray → straight · focus ray → parallel

Use two principal rays to locate the image; ray diagrams are not scale drawings.

BApply it carefully

Worked example

Locate an image using a ray diagram.

From the object tip, draw a parallel ray refracted through F and a central ray undeviated. Their intersection gives the image tip.

Common mix-up

State the condition that applies here, then distinguish the ray, wave pattern and particle motion.

Quick check

Which principal ray passes through a thin lens optical centre without changing direction?

B. The central ray is treated as undeviated for a thin lens.
