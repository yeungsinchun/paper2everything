Book 3 · Syllabus 19

Lenses

Choose a section to review its key idea, diagram and concept check.

  1. 19.1 — Basics of lenses

    A converging lens brings parallel rays to its principal focus. A diverging lens spreads parallel rays as if from a virtual focus.

  2. 19.2 — Images formed by lenses

    A converging lens can form real or virtual images depending on object distance. A diverging lens forms an upright, diminished virtual image.

  3. 19.3 — Graphical methods for lenses

    Use principal rays: parallel then through focus; through optical centre undeviated; through focus then parallel.

  4. 19.4 — Lens formula

    For a thin lens, 1/f = 1/u + 1/v using the stated real-distance convention. Magnification m = image height/object height = v/u.
