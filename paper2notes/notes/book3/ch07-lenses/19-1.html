Basics of lenses

ACore model: Basics of lenses

Key idea

A converging lens brings parallel rays to its principal focus. A diverging lens spreads parallel rays as if from a virtual focus.

f is the distance from the optical centre to the principal focus.

Converging lenses bring parallel rays to a real focus; diverging lenses have a virtual focus.

BApply it carefully

Worked example

Parallel rays reach a converging lens.

After refraction, they meet at the principal focus. The focal length is measured from the optical centre.

Common mix-up

State the condition that applies here, then distinguish the ray, wave pattern and particle motion.
