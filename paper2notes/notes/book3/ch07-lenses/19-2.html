Section 19.2

Images formed by lenses

A converging lens can form real or virtual images depending on object distance. A diverging lens forms an upright, diminished virtual image.

  • AModel and key relation

ACore model: Images formed by lenses

Key idea

A converging lens can form real or virtual images depending on object distance. A diverging lens forms an upright, diminished virtual image.

1/f = 1/u + 1/v

Use the sign and real-distance convention taught for the lens; identify whether the image is real or virtual.

BApply it carefully

Worked example

A converging lens has f = 10 cm and u = 30 cm.

1/v = 1/10 − 1/30 = 1/15, so v = 15 cm. The positive real image can form on a screen.

Common mix-up

State the condition that applies here, then distinguish the ray, wave pattern and particle motion.

Quick check

An object is beyond the focal point of a converging lens. What kind of image can form on a screen?

A. A converging lens forms a real image on the far side when the object is beyond its focus.
