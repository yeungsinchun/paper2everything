Section 16.1

Light

Light is an electromagnetic transverse wave. It travels through vacuum and transfers energy without transporting matter.

Remember

Main ideaLight is an electromagnetic transverse wave. It travels through vacuum and transfers energy without transporting matter.

UseIdentify what remains fixed, what changes, and what the wave carries.

CheckState the relevant physical condition before using a formula.

  • AModel and key relation
  • BApply the idea

ACore model: Light

Key idea

Light is an electromagnetic transverse wave. It travels through vacuum and transfers energy without transporting matter.

Light is an electromagnetic transverse wave.

It can travel through vacuum; its speed in vacuum is c ≈ 3.0 × 10⁸ m s⁻¹.

BApply it carefully

Worked example

Light travels from the Sun to Earth through space.

Light is electromagnetic, so it transfers energy through the vacuum without a material medium.

Common mix-up

State the condition that applies here, then distinguish the ray, wave pattern and particle motion.

Quick check
