Section 16.3

Comparing light and sound

Light and sound both transfer energy as waves, but only sound requires a material medium. Their speeds and particle motions differ.

Remember

Main ideaLight and sound both transfer energy as waves, but only sound requires a material medium. Their speeds and particle motions differ.

UseIdentify what remains fixed, what changes, and what the wave carries.

CheckState the relevant physical condition before using a formula.

  • AModel and key relation
  • BApply the idea

ACore model: Comparing light and sound

Key idea

Light and sound both transfer energy as waves, but only sound requires a material medium. Their speeds and particle motions differ.

Both transfer energy · only sound needs a medium

Compare medium, particle motion and speed rather than treating all waves alike.

BApply it carefully

Worked example

Compare a light wave and a sound wave in a vacuum.

Light travels through vacuum; sound cannot because it needs particles to pass on the vibration.

Common mix-up

State the condition that applies here, then distinguish the ray, wave pattern and particle motion.
