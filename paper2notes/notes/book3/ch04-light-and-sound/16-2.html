Sound

ACore model: Sound

Key idea

Sound is a longitudinal mechanical wave. Compressions and rarefactions travel through a medium; pitch relates to frequency and loudness to amplitude.

v = fλ

Sound is longitudinal; frequency sets pitch and amplitude relates to loudness.

BApply it carefully

Worked example

Sound has f = 500 Hz and λ = 0.68 m.

v = fλ = 500 × 0.68 = 340 m s⁻¹.

Common mix-up

State the condition that applies here, then distinguish the ray, wave pattern and particle motion.

Quick check

A sound wave has frequency 500 Hz and wavelength 0.68 m. What is its speed?

C. Wave speed is frequency times wavelength: 500 × 0.68 = 340 m s⁻¹.
