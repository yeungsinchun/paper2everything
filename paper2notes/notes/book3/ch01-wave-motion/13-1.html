Waves

What a wave is, where it comes from, and what it carries.

Goals

Students should be able to:

  • interpret wave motion in terms of oscillation

  • realise waves as transmitting energy without transferring matter

  • AOscillations make waves
  • BMechanical or electromagnetic, travelling or stationary
  • CEnergy moves, matter stays

AOscillations make waves

Definition

A wave is a disturbance that travels outwards from an oscillating (vibrating) source. A single short disturbance is a pulse. The material the wave travels through is the medium.

  • Every wave starts with something oscillating: a hand on a rope, a finger tapping water, a drum skin, electrons in an aerial.
  • Each part of the medium copies the motion of its neighbour a little later, so the disturbance moves on while each part stays near home.

Quick check

A girl flicks one end of a long rope once. What travels along the rope?

B. Each part of the rope only moves sideways and back. A mixes up the disturbance with the material; C is not the wave on the rope; D needs repeated flicking, not one flick.

BMechanical or electromagnetic, travelling or stationary

Sort byOne kindThe other kind
Needs a medium?Mechanical waves need one: water waves, waves on strings and springs, soundElectromagnetic (EM) waves do not: light, radio, microwaves, X-rays cross a vacuum
Does the pattern move?Travelling waves carry the pattern (and energy) alongStationary waves stay in place, such as a plucked guitar string (Ch.3)

Quick check

(a) Sound can travel through the vacuum of space.

False. Sound is a mechanical wave and needs particles to pass the vibration on.

(b) Sunlight reaches Earth even though most of the journey is through empty space.

True. Light is an electromagnetic wave, so it needs no medium.

CEnergy moves, matter stays

Key idea

A travelling wave transfers energy from one place to another without transferring matter. The particles of the medium oscillate about fixed positions.

Exam tip

“The wave carries the water forward” loses the mark. Say the particles oscillate about their equilibrium positions and only energy is passed on.

Quick check

A cork floats on a pond as ripples pass it. Which statement is correct?

C. The water and the cork oscillate in place. A and D say matter is carried; B forgets that the water does oscillate.
