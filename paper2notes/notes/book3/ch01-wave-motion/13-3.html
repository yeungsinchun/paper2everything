BPeriod, frequency and wave speed

Definition

The period \(T\) is the time for one complete oscillation, which is also the time for the source to make one whole wave. The frequency \(f\) is the number of complete waves made each second, measured in hertz (1 Hz = 1 s−1).

\[ f = \frac{1}{T} \]
\[ v = \frac{\lambda}{T} = f\lambda \]
  • \(v\): wave speed, the distance a crest (or any point of the pattern) travels each second, in m s−1.
  • Why \(v = f\lambda\): in one period the wave moves forward exactly one wavelength, so \(v = \lambda / T\).
Worked example

Worked example

A vibrator makes 30 complete waves on a string in 6.0 s. The crests are 0.40 m apart. Find \(T\), \(f\) and \(v\).

  1. \( T = \dfrac{6.0\ \text{s}}{30} = 0.20\ \text{s} \)
  2. \( f = \dfrac{1}{T} = \dfrac{1}{0.20} = 5.0\ \text{Hz} \)
  3. \( v = f\lambda = 5.0 \times 0.40 = 2.0\ \text{m s}^{-1} \)

Quick check

A violin string vibrates 26 400 times in one minute. What is its frequency?

C: 26 400 ÷ 60 s = 440 Hz. A forgets to divide by the time; B divides by 30 s; D is the period, not the frequency.

CWhat sets the wave speed

ChangeWave speed on a string or spring
Larger tension (stretch it tighter)Faster
Lighter string (less mass per metre)Faster
Higher frequency at the sourceSame speed; \(\lambda\) gets shorter
Bigger amplitude (shake harder)Same speed

The speed of a mechanical wave depends on the medium. The source sets the frequency; the medium sets the speed; \(\lambda = v/f\) then follows.

Going further: the string formula

Going further

For a string of tension \(F\) and mass per unit length \(\mu\) (kg m−1), the wave speed is \( v = \sqrt{F/\mu} \). Four times the tension doubles the speed. A string of the same material but twice the radius has four times the mass per metre, so the wave travels at half the speed.

Quick check

Joe sends waves along a stretched string. Which change makes the waves travel faster?

C. More tension means a faster wave. A and B change the source, not the medium; D makes the string heavier per metre, so the wave is slower.
