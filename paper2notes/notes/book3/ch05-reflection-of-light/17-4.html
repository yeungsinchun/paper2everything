Key idea

Diffuse reflection scatters rays from a rough surface; each local reflection still obeys the law of reflection.

Each ray still obeys i = r

A rough surface has many differently oriented normals, so reflected rays spread.

BApply it carefully

Worked example

A rough wall is illuminated by a narrow beam.

Different local normals send reflected rays in many directions; each ray still obeys i = r.

Common mix-up

State the condition that applies here, then distinguish the ray, wave pattern and particle motion.
