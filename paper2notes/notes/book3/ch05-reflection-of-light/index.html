Book 3 · Syllabus 17

Reflection of Light

Choose a section to review its key idea, diagram and concept check.

  1. 17.1 — Light and vision

    We see an object when light from it enters the eye. A luminous object emits light; a non-luminous object reflects light.

  2. 17.2 — Reflection

    The incident ray, reflected ray and normal lie in one plane. The angles are equal and measured from the normal.

  3. 17.3 — Mirror image

    A plane mirror forms a virtual, upright image the same size as the object, at equal perpendicular distance behind the mirror.

  4. 17.4 — More about reflection

    Diffuse reflection scatters rays from a rough surface; each local reflection still obeys the law of reflection.
