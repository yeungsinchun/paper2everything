ACore model: Reflection

Key idea

The incident ray, reflected ray and normal lie in one plane. The angles are equal and measured from the normal.

i = r

Angles are measured from the normal, not from the mirror surface.

BApply it carefully

Worked example

A ray is 25° from the mirror surface. Find i.

The normal is 90° from the surface, so i = 90° − 25° = 65°.

Common mix-up

State the condition that applies here, then distinguish the ray, wave pattern and particle motion.

Quick check

A ray makes 25° with a plane mirror surface. What is its angle of incidence?

D. The normal is perpendicular to the surface, so the incidence angle is 90° − 25° = 65°.
