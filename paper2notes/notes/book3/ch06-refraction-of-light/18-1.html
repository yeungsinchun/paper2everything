Section 18.1

Refraction

Light changes speed when it enters a different medium. It bends towards the normal on entering a slower medium.

ACore model: Refraction

Key idea

Light changes speed when it enters a different medium. It bends towards the normal on entering a slower medium.

n = c/v

Entering a higher-index medium slows light and bends it towards the normal.

BApply it carefully

Worked example

A ray passes from air into glass.

Glass has a higher refractive index, so light slows and bends towards the normal.

Common mix-up

State the condition that applies here, then distinguish the ray, wave pattern and particle motion.

Quick check

Light enters glass from air and slows down. Which way does the refracted ray bend?

A. Light bends towards the normal when it enters the slower, higher-index medium.
