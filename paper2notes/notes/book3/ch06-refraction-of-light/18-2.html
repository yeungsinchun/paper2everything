Key idea

For a fixed pair of media, sin i / sin r is constant. The refractive index n = c/v, where c is the vacuum speed and v is speed in the medium.

n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r

Angles are measured from the normal; for one pair of media sin i/sin r is constant.

BApply it carefully

Worked example

Light enters glass from air at i = 30°; take n₁ = 1.0 and n₂ = 1.5.

sin r = (1.0/1.5) sin 30° = 1/3, so r ≈ 19.5° from the normal.

Common mix-up

State the condition that applies here, then distinguish the ray, wave pattern and particle motion.

Quick check

For light entering glass from air, which angles go into Snell’s law?

C. Snell’s law uses incidence and refraction angles measured from the normal.
