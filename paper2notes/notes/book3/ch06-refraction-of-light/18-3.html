Section 18.3

Total internal reflection

Total internal reflection needs travel from higher to lower refractive index and incidence above the critical angle; sin C = n₂/n₁.

ACore model: Total internal reflection

Key idea

Total internal reflection needs travel from higher to lower refractive index and incidence above the critical angle; sin C = n₂/n₁.

sin C = n₂/n₁ (n₁ > n₂)

C is the critical angle; total internal reflection requires incidence greater than C.

BApply it carefully

Worked example

For glass-to-air, take n₁ = 1.5 and n₂ = 1.0.

sin C = 1.0/1.5, so C ≈ 41.8°. Incidence greater than this gives total internal reflection.

Common mix-up

State the condition that applies here, then distinguish the ray, wave pattern and particle motion.

Quick check

Light travels from glass into air. Its incidence angle exceeds the critical angle. What happens?

D. The higher-to-lower index condition and i > C produce total internal reflection.
