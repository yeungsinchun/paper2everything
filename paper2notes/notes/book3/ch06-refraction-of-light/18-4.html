Key idea

Optical fibres guide light by repeated total internal reflection.

Worked example

State the conditions for light to be guided by total internal reflection.

Light must travel from higher to lower refractive index and strike the boundary above the critical angle.

State the condition that applies here, then distinguish the ray, wave pattern and particle motion.
