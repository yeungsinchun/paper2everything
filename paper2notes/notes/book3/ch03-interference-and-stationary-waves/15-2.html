Section 15.2

Stationary waves

Two similar waves travelling in opposite directions form nodes with zero displacement and antinodes with maximum displacement.

Remember

Main ideaTwo similar waves travelling in opposite directions form nodes with zero displacement and antinodes with maximum displacement.

UseIdentify what remains fixed, what changes, and what the wave carries.

CheckState the relevant physical condition before using a formula.

  • AModel and key relation
  • BApply the idea

ACore model: Stationary waves

Key idea

Two similar waves travelling in opposite directions form nodes with zero displacement and antinodes with maximum displacement.

Adjacent nodes are λ/2 apart · node to adjacent antinode is λ/4

A stationary pattern has fixed nodes and antinodes.

BApply it carefully

Worked example

Adjacent nodes are 0.30 m apart.

Node spacing is λ/2, so λ = 2 × 0.30 = 0.60 m.

Common mix-up

State the condition that applies here, then distinguish the ray, wave pattern and particle motion.

Quick check

A stationary wave has neighbouring nodes 0.30 m apart. What is its wavelength?

C. Adjacent nodes are separated by half a wavelength.
