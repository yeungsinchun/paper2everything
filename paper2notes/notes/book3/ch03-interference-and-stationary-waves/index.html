Book 3 · Syllabus 15

Interference and Stationary Waves

Choose a section to review its key idea, diagram and concept check.

  1. 15.1 — Superposition

    When waves overlap, the resultant displacement is the algebraic sum of their individual displacements.

  2. 15.2 — Stationary waves

    Two similar waves travelling in opposite directions form nodes with zero displacement and antinodes with maximum displacement.

  3. 15.3 — Interference

    Coherent sources have the same frequency and a constant phase difference. Path difference determines constructive or destructive interference.
