Key idea

When waves overlap, the resultant displacement is the algebraic sum of their individual displacements.

y = y₁ + y₂

Add the signed displacements at the same position and instant.

BApply it carefully

Worked example

Two overlapping displacements are +3 cm and −1 cm.

The resultant is +3 + (−1) = +2 cm. Opposite signs mean partial cancellation.

Common mix-up

State the condition that applies here, then distinguish the ray, wave pattern and particle motion.

Quick check

Two pulses meet at a point. Their displacements there are +3 cm and −1 cm. What is the resultant displacement?

B. Superposition adds signed displacements: +3 + (−1) = +2 cm.
