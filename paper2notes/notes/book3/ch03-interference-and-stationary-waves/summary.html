Chapter 3 summary

Interference and Stationary Waves

Key ideas

  • Superposition. When waves overlap, the resultant displacement is the algebraic sum of their individual displacements.
  • Stationary waves. Two similar waves travelling in opposite directions form nodes with zero displacement and antinodes with maximum displacement.
  • Interference. Coherent sources have the same frequency and a constant phase difference. Path difference determines constructive or destructive interference.

Formula sheet

y = y₁ + y₂; adjacent nodes are λ/2 apart

y is resultant displacement; y₁ and y₂ are individual signed displacements; λ is wavelength.

Definitions to recall

Superposition: overlapping displacements add. Node: zero-displacement point. Antinode: maximum-displacement point. Coherent: same frequency and fixed phase difference.
