ACore model: Refraction

Key idea

A wave changes direction when its speed changes across a boundary. Frequency stays fixed; wavelength changes because v = fλ.

v = fλ · f stays constant at a stationary boundary

A speed change changes wavelength and may change direction.

BApply it carefully

Worked example

A wave slows from 6.0 to 4.0 m s⁻¹ while f = 10 Hz stays fixed.

λ₁ = 6.0/10 = 0.60 m and λ₂ = 4.0/10 = 0.40 m. Frequency stays fixed at a stationary boundary.

Common mix-up

State the condition that applies here, then distinguish the ray, wave pattern and particle motion.

Quick check

A wave crosses into a region where it travels more slowly. Its source frequency is unchanged. What happens to its wavelength?

A. Since v = fλ and f is fixed, a lower speed means a shorter wavelength.
