Section 14.4

Diffraction

Waves spread after passing an opening or edge. Spreading is strongest when the gap is comparable to the wavelength.

ACore model: Diffraction

Key idea

Waves spread after passing an opening or edge. Spreading is strongest when the gap is comparable to the wavelength.

Strong diffraction when gap width ≈ wavelength

A much wider gap gives less spreading.

BApply it carefully

Worked example

Compare openings 0.20 m and 2.0 m wide for λ = 0.20 m.

The 0.20 m opening gives much stronger spreading because its width is comparable to λ.

Common mix-up

State the condition that applies here, then distinguish the ray, wave pattern and particle motion.

Quick check

Which opening gives the strongest spreading for the same wave?

C. Diffraction is strongest when the gap is comparable to wavelength.
