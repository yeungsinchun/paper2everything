ACore model: Reflection

Key idea

At a boundary, the reflected wave obeys angle of incidence = angle of reflection, measured from the normal. Reflection can reverse a pulse at a fixed end.

i = r

Measure incidence i and reflection r from the normal; at a fixed end, a reflected pulse is inverted.

BApply it carefully

Worked example

A ray strikes a mirror at 35° to the normal.

The reflected ray is also 35° from the normal, on the opposite side of it.

Common mix-up

State the condition that applies here, then distinguish the ray, wave pattern and particle motion.

Quick check

A wave strikes a straight barrier at 35° to the normal. What is its reflection angle?

B. The angle of reflection equals the angle of incidence, measured from the normal.
