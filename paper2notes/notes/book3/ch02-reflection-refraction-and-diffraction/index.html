Book 3 · Syllabus 14

Reflection, Refraction and Diffraction

Choose a section to review its key idea, diagram and concept check.

  1. 14.1 — Illustrating waves

    Wavefronts join points in the same phase; rays show the direction of travel and meet wavefronts at right angles.

  2. 14.2 — Reflection

    At a boundary, the reflected wave obeys angle of incidence = angle of reflection, measured from the normal. Reflection can reverse a pulse at a fixed end.

  3. 14.3 — Refraction

    A wave changes direction when its speed changes across a boundary. Frequency stays fixed; wavelength changes because v = fλ.

  4. 14.4 — Diffraction

    Waves spread after passing an opening or edge. Spreading is strongest when the gap is comparable to the wavelength.
