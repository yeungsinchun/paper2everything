Chapter 27

Summary: Nuclear Energy

Fission breaks a heavy nucleus with a slow neutron; fusion joins light nuclei with high kinetic energy; both weigh less after and pay that mass as \( \Delta E = \Delta mc^{2} \).

4Traps that cost marks

Fission is not decay

Decay: spontaneous, half-life, fixed products. Fission: neutron-induced, controllable, varied products + neutrons.

Chain needs ≥1 neutron per fission on average

Not energy, not number of products. Average \(k\) matters; cutting a sphere separates next targets → \(k\) drops.

Reactor ≠ bomb enrichment

Reactor ~3% U-235 cannot become bomb even if uncontrolled; bomb needs >90% plus compression.

Per reaction vs per kilogram

Single fission (200 MeV) > single D–T fusion (17.6 MeV). But 1 kg fusion fuel gives ~5× fission. Do not flip them.

Fusion not started by neutrons

Fission needs slow neutron. Fusion needs high KE/Temperature to beat Coulomb hill — no slow-neutron capture.

Only Δm counts, not total m

\(E =\Delta m c^{2}\) uses mass defect, not total mass. Also 1 MeV =\(10^{6}\) eV.

Units that can be energy

\( \mathrm{u} \) and \( \mathrm{eV} \) can (via \(c^{2}\)). \( \mathrm{Bq} \) is activity, not energy. 1 u is 1/12 C-12 mass, not neutron mass.

Sun mass loss

\(\Delta m = P/c^{2}\): power over \(c^{2}\) per second, not per year. \(c\) squared matters.

Concept check

Which must be correct for a fission reaction?

  1. U-235 is split
  2. Triggered by neutrons
  3. Lighter nuclei are produced

B. Any fission produces lighter fragments (3) and is neutron-triggered (2). Not necessarily U-235 — other heavy nuclei fission too.
