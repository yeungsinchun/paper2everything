How the DSE practice gets here

From a fresh checkout:

  • ./paper2db/pipeline --force --yes — rebuilds the classified banks from paper2db/paper/ PDFs into paper2db/tests/sections/{mc,lq} and paper2db/tests/reconstructed/ (gitignored).

  • ./paper2notes/scripts/sync-dse.sh — copies only the needed per-section crops into gitignored paper2notes/notes/_local/dse/{mc,lq}/<section> and also into notes/book*/_local/dse/ for the section pages to find via relative ../_local/dse/… links. Generated crops stay gitignored.

  • Then python3 -m http.server --directory paper2notes/notes 8000 — open 25.1 and the DSE deck at the bottom is populated.

Book 5 Ch.1–3 intake lives in notes/_source/book5-ch0{1,2,3}/ (ocr.md / outline.md / problems.md / images/INDEX.md). QB banks are canonical in paper2db/qb/; run ./paper2db/scripts/convert-qb-to-pdf.sh for QB PDFs.
