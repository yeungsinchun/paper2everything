Chapter 4 summary

Light and Sound

Key ideas

  • Light. Light is an electromagnetic transverse wave. It travels through vacuum and transfers energy without transporting matter.
  • Sound. Sound is a longitudinal mechanical wave. Compressions and rarefactions travel through a medium; pitch relates to frequency and loudness to amplitude.
  • Comparing light and sound. Light and sound both transfer energy as waves, but only sound requires a material medium. Their speeds and particle motions differ.

Formula sheet

v = fλ; c ≈ 3.0 × 10⁸ m s⁻¹ in vacuum

v is wave speed, f frequency, λ wavelength, and c the speed of light in vacuum.

Definitions to recall

Light: transverse electromagnetic wave. Sound: longitudinal mechanical wave. Frequency determines pitch; amplitude relates to loudness.
