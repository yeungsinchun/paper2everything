4.1LightLight is an electromagnetic transverse wave. It travels through vacuum and transfers energy without transporting matter.4.2SoundSound is a longitudinal mechanical wave. Compressions and rarefactions travel through a medium; pitch relates to frequency and loudness to amplitude.4.3Comparing light and soundLight and sound both transfer energy as waves, but only sound requires a material medium. Their speeds and particle motions differ.
