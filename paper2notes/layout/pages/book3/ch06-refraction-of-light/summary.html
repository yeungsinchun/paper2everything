Chapter 6 summary

Refraction of Light

Key ideas

  • Refraction. Light changes speed when it enters a different medium. It bends towards the normal on entering a slower medium.
  • Laws of refraction. For a fixed pair of media, sin i / sin r is constant. The refractive index n = c/v, where c is the vacuum speed and v is speed in the medium.
  • Total internal reflection. Total internal reflection needs travel from higher to lower refractive index and incidence above the critical angle; sin C = n₂/n₁.
  • More phenomena and applications. Refraction explains apparent depth and mirages; total internal reflection guides light in optical fibres.

Formula sheet

n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r; sin C = n₂/n₁ when n₁ > n₂

n is refractive index; i and r are measured from the normal; C is the critical angle.

Definitions to recall

Refraction: direction change due to speed change. Critical angle: incidence angle for a refracted ray at 90°.
