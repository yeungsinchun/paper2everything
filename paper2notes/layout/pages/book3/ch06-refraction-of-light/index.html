6.1RefractionLight changes speed when it enters a different medium. It bends towards the normal on entering a slower medium.6.2Laws of refractionFor a fixed pair of media, sin i / sin r is constant. The refractive index n = c/v, where c is the vacuum speed and v is speed in the medium.6.3Total internal reflectionTotal internal reflection needs travel from higher to lower refractive index and incidence above the critical angle; sin C = n₂/n₁.6.4More phenomena and applicationsRefraction explains apparent depth and mirages; total internal reflection guides light in optical fibres.
