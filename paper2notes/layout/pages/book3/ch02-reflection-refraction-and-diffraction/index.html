2.1Illustrating wavesWavefronts join points in the same phase; rays show the direction of travel and meet wavefronts at right angles.2.2ReflectionAt a boundary, the reflected wave obeys angle of incidence = angle of reflection, measured from the normal. Reflection can reverse a pulse at a fixed end.2.3RefractionA wave changes direction when its speed changes across a boundary. Frequency stays fixed; wavelength changes because v = fλ.2.4DiffractionWaves spread after passing an opening or edge. Spreading is strongest when the gap is comparable to the wavelength.
