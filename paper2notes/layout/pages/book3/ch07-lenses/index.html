Lenses

Choose a section to review its key idea, diagram and concept check.

7.1Basics of lensesA converging lens brings parallel rays to its principal focus. A diverging lens spreads parallel rays as if from a virtual focus.7.2Images formed by lensesA converging lens can form real or virtual images depending on object distance. A diverging lens forms an upright, diminished virtual image.7.3Graphical methods for lensesUse principal rays: parallel then through focus; through optical centre undeviated; through focus then parallel.7.4Lens formulaFor a thin lens, 1/f = 1/u + 1/v using the stated real-distance convention. Magnification m = image height/object height = v/u.
