Chapter 7 summary

Lenses

Key ideas

  • Basics of lenses. A converging lens brings parallel rays to its principal focus. A diverging lens spreads parallel rays as if from a virtual focus.
  • Images formed by lenses. A converging lens can form real or virtual images depending on object distance. A diverging lens forms an upright, diminished virtual image.
  • Graphical methods for lenses. Use principal rays: parallel then through focus; through optical centre undeviated; through focus then parallel.
  • Lens formula. For a thin lens, 1/f = 1/u + 1/v using the stated real-distance convention. Magnification m = image height/object height = v/u.

Formula sheet

1/f = 1/u + 1/v; m = v/u = hᵢ/hₒ

f is focal length, u object distance, v image distance, m magnification; hᵢ and hₒ are image and object heights.

Definitions to recall

Principal focus: point where axial parallel rays meet or appear to diverge from. Real image: formed by converging rays; virtual image: formed by diverging rays traced back.
