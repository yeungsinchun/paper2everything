5.1Light and visionWe see an object when light from it enters the eye. A luminous object emits light; a non-luminous object reflects light.5.2ReflectionThe incident ray, reflected ray and normal lie in one plane. The angles are equal and measured from the normal.5.3Mirror imageA plane mirror forms a virtual, upright image the same size as the object, at equal perpendicular distance behind the mirror.5.4More about reflectionDiffuse reflection scatters rays from a rough surface; each local reflection still obeys the law of reflection.
