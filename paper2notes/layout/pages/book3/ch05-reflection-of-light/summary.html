Chapter 5 summary

Reflection of Light

Key ideas

  • Light and vision. We see an object when light from it enters the eye. A luminous object emits light; a non-luminous object reflects light.
  • Reflection. The incident ray, reflected ray and normal lie in one plane. The angles are equal and measured from the normal.
  • Mirror image. A plane mirror forms a virtual, upright image the same size as the object, at equal perpendicular distance behind the mirror.
  • More about reflection. Diffuse reflection scatters rays from a rough surface; each local reflection still obeys the law of reflection.

Formula sheet

i = r

i and r are incidence and reflection angles measured from the normal.

Definitions to recall

Luminous: emits light. Virtual image: apparent image from diverging rays. Diffuse reflection: reflected rays spread from a rough surface.
