Chapter summary

Orbital motions under gravity

The relationships and distinctions to recall before solving problems.

Formula sheet

T² = 4π²a³/(GM); v = √(GM/r); U = −GMm/r; vesc = √(2GM/R).

Check units and conditions before substituting values.

Definitions

  • Perihelion — closest point to the Sun.
  • Aphelion — farthest point from the Sun.
  • Semi-major axis — half the longest diameter of an ellipse.
  • Escape speed — minimum speed for non-negative total energy.
  • Apparent weight — support force on an object.

Key ideas

  • 3.1 · Kepler’s laws — Elliptical orbits, equal areas and the period–size relation.
  • 3.2 · Orbital motion under gravity — Circular orbital speed, period and central mass.
  • 3.3 · Energy in orbital motion — Gravitational potential energy, orbital energy and escape.
  • 3.4 · Apparent weightlessness — Shared free fall explains weightlessness in orbit.
Common mistakes

Use SI units with G. T² = a³ applies only to solar orbits in years and AU.
