\question{8}{8} On a certain day, the temperature at $2$ p.m.\ is $25^{\circ}$C. The temperature changes after each hour from $2$ p.m.\ to $5$ p.m.\ are recorded as follows (A decrease in temperature is represented by a negative number.)

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|}
\hline
\textbf{Time} & \textbf{Temperature change ($^{\circ}$C)} \\
\hline
$3$ p.m. & $+1.3$ \\
\hline
$4$ p.m. & $-0.4$ \\
\hline
$5$ p.m. & $-1.1$ \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{subparts}
  \item Find the temperatures at
  \begin{subpartsii}[1]
    \item $3$ p.m.,
    \item $4$ p.m.,
    \item $5$ p.m.
  \end{subpartsii}

  \item If the temperature at $7$ p.m.\ is $22.6^{\circ}$C and the temperature changes from $5$ p.m.\ to $6$ p.m.\ and $6$ p.m.\ to $7$ p.m.\ are the same, Candy claims that the temperature at $6$ p.m.\ is higher than the temperature at $2$ p.m. Do you agree? Explain your answer.
\end{subparts}
