\begin{subparts}
  \item Kelvin answered $42$ questions and at least $30$ of the questions that Kelvin answered are correct. Find Kelvin's
  \begin{subpartsii}
    \item the highest possible score,
    \item the lowest possible score.
  \end{subpartsii}
  \item Mathias did not answer $8$ questions and he is confident that he will get at least $28$ questions correct. Kelvin claims that he must get a higher score than Mathias. Do you agree? Explain your answer.
\end{subparts}
