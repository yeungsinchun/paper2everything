\question{7}{8} The following shows a sequence of figures formed by dots.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{cccc}
$\substack{\bullet\ \bullet \\ \bullet\ \phantom{\bullet}}$ &
$\substack{\bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet \\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet \\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \phantom{\bullet}}$ &
$\substack{\bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet \\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet \\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet \\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \phantom{\bullet}}$ &
$\substack{\bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet \\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet \\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet \\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet \\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \bullet\ \phantom{\bullet}}$ \\
1st & 2nd & 3rd & 4th \\
\end{tabular}
\qquad $\cdots$
\end{center}

It is given that the $n$th figure has $[(n+1)^2-1]$ dots.

\begin{subparts}
  \item How many dots are there in the 12th figure?
  \item Is there a figure formed by $190$ dots? Explain your answer.
  \item Does the 19th figure have an even number of dots? Explain your answer.
\end{subparts}

\answerlines{14}
