\question{7}{8} Three teams begin training together on 6 September. Team A trains every $12$ days, Team B every $18$ days and Team C every $30$ days.
\begin{subparts}
  \item Find the number of days before all three teams train together again.
  \item Find the date on which they next train together.
  \item During the next $365$ days after 6 September, how many further dates do all three teams train together?
\end{subparts}
\answerlines{16}
