\question{1}{8} A recipe uses $2\frac{1}{4}$ kg of flour for one batch of bread. A bakery opens a $6$ kg bag of flour and makes $1.6$ batches in the morning and $\frac{2}{3}$ of a batch in the afternoon.
\begin{subparts}
  \item Find the total mass of flour used.
  \item Find the mass of flour left in the bag.
  \item The remaining flour is packed equally into $3$ containers. Find the mass in each container.
\end{subparts}
