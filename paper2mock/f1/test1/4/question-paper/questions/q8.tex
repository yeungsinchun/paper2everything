\question{8}{10} Sea level is represented by $0$ m. A diver starts at $+6$ m on a platform. She descends $18$ m, rises $7$ m, descends $11$ m, and then rises $x$ m.
\begin{subparts}
  \item Find her position after the first three movements.
  \item She finishes above sea level. Write an inequality for $x$ and state the least whole-number value of $x$.
  \item The diver claims that a rise of $23$ m would put her exactly at sea level. Is her claim correct? Explain.
  \item She returns to the platform from her position after the first three movements. State the distance and direction of her final movement.
\end{subparts}
