\question{6}{8} Three ribbons are $84$ cm, $108$ cm and $132$ cm long. They are cut into equal greatest-possible lengths, with no ribbon left over.
\begin{subparts}
  \item Find the length of each piece.
  \item Find the total number of pieces.
  \item A fourth ribbon is to be cut into the same length pieces and must leave a remainder of $5$ cm. Its length is between $90$ cm and $120$ cm. Find all possible lengths of the fourth ribbon.
\end{subparts}
