\question{2}{7} Leon's bank balance is $-\$48$ on Monday morning. The following transactions are made.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
\textbf{Transaction} & Deposit & Withdrawal & Deposit & Withdrawal\\ \hline
\textbf{Amount} & $\$75$ & $\$36$ & $\$20$ & $\$19$\\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\begin{subparts}
  \item Write a directed-number expression for the final balance and find it.
  \item At what stage does Leon's balance first become positive?
  \item A bank charge of $\$c$ is then deducted. The balance becomes less than $-\$20$. Write an inequality for $c$ and state the least whole-number value of $c$.
\end{subparts}
