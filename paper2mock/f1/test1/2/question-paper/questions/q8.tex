\question{8}{10} The following table shows the positions of three locations relative to a school. Positions to the east of the school are taken as positive.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|l|c|c|c|}
\hline
\textbf{Location} & Library & Cinema & Home \\
\hline
\textbf{Position} & $-250$ m & $+420$ m & $+180$ m \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{subparts}
  \item Find the directed distance from the Library to the Cinema.

  \item It is known that a bus stop is between $300$ m and $400$ m east of the Library.
  \begin{subpartsii}[1]
    \item Is the Cinema to the east of the bus stop? Explain your answer.
    \item Sam claims that the Home must be to the west of the Cinema. Do you agree? Explain your answer.
  \end{subpartsii}

  \item A student leaves the school, walks $200$ m east, then $350$ m west, and finally $120$ m east.
  \begin{subpartsii}[1]
    \item Find the final position of the student relative to the school.
    \item How far and in which direction must the student walk to return to the school?
  \end{subpartsii}
\end{subparts}

\answerlines{13}
