\question{8}{10} A bus starts a route with $38$ passengers. The table shows the numbers who board and alight at the next three stops.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
\textbf{Stop} & A & B & C \\
\hline
\textbf{Board} & $17$ & $8$ & $14$ \\
\hline
\textbf{Alight} & $6$ & $21$ & $19$ \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{subparts}
  \item Find the number of passengers on the bus after each stop.
  \item Find the net change in the number of passengers from the start of the route to after Stop C. Interpret your answer.
  \item At which stop is the decrease in passengers greatest? Show how you decide.
  \item Find the total number of passengers who alight at the three stops.
\end{subparts}

\answerlines{14}
