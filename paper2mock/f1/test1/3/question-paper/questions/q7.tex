\question{7}{8} Cuboids of dimensions $6$ cm by $9$ cm by $15$ cm are available. Identical cuboids are packed, without gaps or cutting, to make the smallest possible cube.
\begin{subparts}
  \item Find the side length of the cube.
  \item Find the number of cuboids along each edge of the cube.
  \item Find the least number of cuboids required.
\end{subparts}
