\question{8}{10} A quiz team starts with $0$ points. In each round, a correct answer gains the stated points and an incorrect answer loses the stated points.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
\textbf{Round} & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4\\ \hline
\textbf{Score change} & $+18$ & $-25$ & $+32$ & $-14$\\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\begin{subparts}
  \item Complete the running-score table.
  \[
  \begin{array}{c|cccc}
  \text{End of round} & 1&2&3&4\\ \hline
  \text{Score}&&&&
  \end{array}
  \]
  \item In round 5, the team loses $x$ points and finishes with a positive score. Write an inequality for $x$ and find the greatest possible integer value of $x$.
  \item Mei says, ``The team gained more points than it lost over the five rounds.'' Is Mei necessarily correct? Explain.
\end{subparts}
\answerlines{14}
