\question{4}{6} The length, width and height of Kevin's bedroom are $4a$ m, $3a$ m and $3$ m respectively. Kevin is going to paint the rectangular ceiling and four rectangular walls of his bedroom. A window of area $\dfrac{a}{2}\,\text{m}^2$ and a door of area $\dfrac{7a}{2}\,\text{m}^2$ which are built on the walls will not be painted.

\begin{subparts}
  \item Express the total painted area in terms of $a$.
  \item If $a=2$ and the cost of painting is $\$50/\text{m}^2$, can Kevin paint his bedroom at a cost of not more than $\$6500$? Explain your answer.
\end{subparts}
