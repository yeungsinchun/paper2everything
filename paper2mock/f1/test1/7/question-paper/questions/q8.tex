\question{8}{8} On a certain day, the temperature at $1$ p.m.\ is $22^\circ\text{C}$. The temperature changes after each hour from $1$ p.m.\ to $4$ p.m.\ are recorded as follows: (A decrease in temperature is represented by a negative number.)

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
\textbf{Time} & $2$ p.m. & $3$ p.m. & $4$ p.m. \\
\hline
\textbf{Temperature change ($^\circ\text{C}$)} & $+2.1$ & $-0.7$ & $-1.4$ \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{subparts}
  \item Find the temperatures at
  \begin{subpartsii}
    \item $2$ p.m.,
    \item $3$ p.m.,
    \item $4$ p.m.
  \end{subpartsii}
  \item If the temperature at $6$ p.m.\ is $20.8^\circ\text{C}$ and the temperature changes from $4$ p.m.\ to $5$ p.m.\ and $5$ p.m.\ to $6$ p.m.\ are the same, Candy claims that the temperature at $5$ p.m.\ is higher than the temperature at $1$ p.m. Do you agree? Explain your answer.
\end{subparts}
