\begin{subparts}
  \item Use directed numbers to write an expression to represent the following and find its value.

  Divide the sum of positive $7$ and negative $5$ by the product of $3$ and $4$.

  \item The height of Amy is $172$ cm and `$+1$ cm' means $1$ cm taller than Amy. What is the height represented by each of the following directed numbers?
  \begin{subpartsii}
    \item $+5$ cm
    \item $-7.5$ cm
  \end{subpartsii}
\end{subparts}
