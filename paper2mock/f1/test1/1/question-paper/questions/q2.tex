\question{2}{7} Answer the following questions.

\begin{subparts}
  \item Write down the expression for ``Subtract the product of negative $3$ and the difference between positive $8$ and negative $6$ from the quotient of the sum of positive $15$ and negative $9$ by negative $2$''.

  \item Alan, Ben and Cindy are 3 students in a class. It is given that Alan is 3 kg above the average weight of the students in the class, Ben is 4 kg lighter than Alan and Cindy is
  4 kg lighter than Ben. Suppose +1 kg means 1 kg above the average weight of the students in the class.
  \begin{subpartsii}
    \item Use directed numbers to represent the weights of Alan, Ben and Cindy after comparing with the average weight of the students in the class.
    \item Is the average weight of Alan, Ben and Cindy greater than the average weight of the students in the class by 1 kg? Explain your answer.
  \end{subpartsii}
\end{subparts}
